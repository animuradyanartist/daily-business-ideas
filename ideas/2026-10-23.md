# The Independent Home Health Agency's "CMS-Ready" Electronic Visit Verification (EVV) & Billing Hub

_2026-10-23 · conviction: high · score: 95/100_

## The idea
A flat-rate SaaS platform for independent home health agencies with 10-100 caregivers, designed to ensure Electronic Visit Verification (EVV) compliance and streamline billing to prevent payment denials and audit risks. For a monthly fee of $100-$500, the hub provides a caregiver mobile application for GPS-verified visit capture, an administrator portal for real-time compliance monitoring, and generates audit-ready reports for seamless submission to state Medicaid/Medicare systems, ensuring agencies get paid accurately and on time.

## Who pays and why
The buyer is the owner or administrator of an independent home health agency, typically managing 10-100 caregivers, primarily serving Medicaid and Medicare patients. These buyers are found in industry associations (e.g., state home care associations), online forums for home health administrators, and by searching for solutions to "EVV compliance," "Medicaid billing denials," or "home health audit readiness." They pay because EVV non-compliance directly leads to payment denials, delayed reimbursements, and severe audit risks, which are existential threats to their agency's financial viability and operational continuity.

## Problem proof
The pain of EVV non-compliance is frequent, severe, and directly impacts an agency's ability to generate revenue.

*   "If the visit record is incomplete or does not match the claim, payment can be delayed or denied. That creates several downstream issues: Claims require manual correction. Accounts receivable grows. Payroll may not align with verified hours. Office staff spend more time chasing documentation."
*   "The most common reason for EVV claim denials in 2026? The most common reason is a “Record Not Found” error. This happens when the data in your EVV system does not perfectly match the data on your billing claim (such as mismatched service codes or dates), causing the state aggregator to reject the match."
*   "If your compliance score falls below the mandated level (e.g., 80% in Texas or 50% in Minnesota), you will typically receive a notice of non-compliance and be required to submit a Corrective Action Plan (CAP). Continued failure can lead to payment suspensions or termination from the Medicaid network."

Medicaid home care claims denial rates run 10–15% industry-wide, resulting in 40–60 denials per month for an agency with 30 patients submitting 400–500 claims. Each denial requires 30–50 minutes to rework, and most agencies only manage to rework about a third of them, leading to permanent revenue losses for services already rendered. Audits have revealed significant payment issues, with a November 2024 audit by the New York State Comptroller finding $14.5 billion in Medicaid personal care claims paid without a matching EVV record, and $97.6 million for home health care claims lacking EVV records. Non-compliance can lead to payment denials, state sanctions, fines, and even loss of licensure. This financial drain and administrative burden occurs daily as caregivers complete visits and billing cycles run.

## Why now
The urgency for an "CMS-Ready" EVV & Billing Hub is driven by escalating enforcement and tightening compliance thresholds in 2026.

1.  **Escalating State Enforcement and "Hard Edits":** In 2026, states are increasingly moving away from "soft edits," where minor EVV data errors might still result in payment, to "hard edits" that trigger automatic claim denials. This shift means that any discrepancy between EVV records and billing claims now carries an immediate and direct financial penalty, making robust, accurate EVV systems critical.
2.  **Rising Compliance Rate Thresholds:** Many states now mandate high EVV compliance rates, often 85% or higher, for submitted claims (e.g., Pennsylvania, Michigan, Texas, Minnesota). Agencies falling below these thresholds face corrective action plans, payment withholding, or even termination from Medicaid networks, creating a strong impetus to adopt reliable solutions.
3.  **Aggressive Federal Scrutiny:** The Department of Health and Human Services Office of Inspector General (HHS-OIG) is actively mining EVV data to identify improper Medicaid payments and pursue massive recoupments. This heightened federal oversight means agencies with inconsistent or incomplete EVV records face drastically increased audit and recoupment risks, making proactive compliance a top priority.
4.  **Increasing Payment Pressure:** The U.S. Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) estimated a 2.4% increase in Medicare home health payment rates for 2026, but this comes with increased scrutiny on documentation. This payment pressure amplifies the value of clean documentation and denial prevention, as agencies cannot afford lost revenue from non-compliant visits.

## Scope of work

#### MVP Cut
The initial version of the "CMS-Ready" EVV & Billing Hub will be laser-focused on solving the most acute pain points for independent home health agencies: compliant Electronic Visit Verification (EVV) data capture and the ability to generate accurate records for billing and audit purposes.

**In Scope for v1:**

*   **Caregiver Mobile Application:** Intuitive interface for caregivers to clock-in and clock-out of client visits, including GPS location verification at the start and end of each visit. This is the core EVV functionality.
*   **Client Management:** Basic profiles for clients, linking them to scheduled visits and associated caregivers.
*   **Caregiver Management:** Basic profiles for caregivers, linking them to clients and visits.
*   **Agency Administrator Web Portal:** A secure web interface for agency owners/administrators to:
    *   View real-time and historical visit data.
    *   Manage client and caregiver profiles.
    *   Identify non-compliant visits (e.g., missed clock-ins, out-of-geofence) with real-time alerts.
    *   Generate reports of completed visits, including EVV data, in a standardized format (e.g., CSV, or a common API export if a specific state aggregator is dominant) suitable for submission to state Medicaid/Medicare billing systems.
*   **User Authentication & Authorization:** Secure login for caregivers and administrators with appropriate role-based access controls.
*   **Basic Reporting:** Essential reports to track visit compliance and identify potential billing issues.

**Out of Scope for v1 (Future Enhancements):**

*   **Advanced Scheduling:** Complex recurring schedules, caregiver availability management, shift bidding, or automated client-caregiver matching.
*   **Direct Billing Integration:** Automated submission of claims directly to state Medicaid/Medicare systems or clearinghouses. The MVP will provide the data *for* billing, not perform the billing itself.
*   **Clinical Documentation/EHR:** Any features related to patient health records, care plans, or medical notes.
*   **Payroll Integration:** Direct integration with third-party payroll systems.
*   **Advanced Analytics & Business Intelligence:** Sophisticated dashboards beyond basic compliance reporting.
*   **Offline Mode:** Initial focus will be on environments with reliable connectivity.

**Rationale for MVP Cut:**
This MVP prioritizes the existential problem of EVV compliance and payment assurance. Agencies *must* capture accurate EVV data to get paid by Medicaid and Medicare. By focusing on this core, the solution directly addresses payment denials and audit risks, offering immediate, quantifiable value. Features like advanced scheduling or direct billing, while valuable, introduce significant complexity and development time without directly solving the primary "get paid, stay compliant" problem that drives initial adoption.
