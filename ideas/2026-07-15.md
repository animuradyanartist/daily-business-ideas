# The freight broker's "carrier compliance" evidence locker

_2026-07-15 · conviction: high · score: 98/100_

## The idea
A flat-rate SaaS tool for small-to-midsize US freight brokerages that automates carrier onboarding and provides continuous, audit-proof compliance monitoring. For a monthly fee of ~$250, the brokerage gets a dashboard that integrates with FMCSA and insurance databases to provide a single, real-time "go/no-go" signal for any carrier. The system automates the collection of insurance certificates, operating authority, and safety ratings, flags fraudulent activity like identity theft, and maintains a timestamped log of every check. This directly mitigates the existential risk of "negligent entrustment" lawsuits and catastrophic financial loss from fraud.

## Who pays and why
The buyer is the owner or operations manager of a US freight brokerage with 5-50 employees. This person is legally and financially responsible for ensuring every carrier they hire is compliant and legitimate. They can be found in communities like the r/freightbrokers subreddit or industry groups on LinkedIn. The cost of failure is not theoretical; a single fraudulent pickup can result in a six-figure cargo loss, and a lawsuit from an accident involving a non-compliant carrier can bankrupt the company. They currently use a combination of manual checks and legacy data services, but these tools are often clunky, require significant manual interpretation, and don't adequately address the rise in sophisticated carrier fraud. The motivation to buy is driven by the need to mitigate catastrophic, uninsurable risk and to reduce the manual, error-prone workload on their team.

Caribe Transport II, LLC* affirmed that brokers can be held liable under state negligence laws. This decision dismantled a long-standing legal defense, making a documented, defensible carrier vetting process an immediate, existential necessity for every broker in the US.
2.  **Exponential Growth in Sophisticated Fraud (April 2026):** The FBI has officially warned the industry about a 60% year-over-year increase in cyber-enabled cargo theft, with losses approaching three-quarters of a billion dollars in 2025. Legacy tools designed to check static compliance data are insufficient against modern identity theft and strategic fraud, creating a clear product gap.

## Scope of work
*   **MVP Scope (In):**
    *   Integration with the FMCSA SAFER API to pull carrier authority, safety, and insurance data.
    *   A simple dashboard to enter an MC/DOT number and get a clear "Go / No-Go / Review" status based on configurable rules.
    *   An immutable, timestamped log of every check performed for audit defense.
    *   Automated, continuous monitoring of saved carriers with email alerts for status changes (e.g., insurance lapse).
*   **MVP Scope (Out):**
    *   Direct insurance certificate OCR and validation.
    *   Advanced fraud detection (IP logging, phone number verification).
    *   Full carrier onboarding packet management (contracts, W9s).
    *   TMS integrations.
*   **Timeline:** 8-12 weeks for a skilled developer to build and launch the MVP.
*   **Resources:** One full-stack developer. Initial capital for data APIs and marketing spend (~$5k). The critical resource is domain expertise to correctly define the compliance rules.

## Risks and kill criteria
1.  **Risk: Inability to acquire customers.** The inertia of legacy tools is too strong, and brokers are too slow to adopt new technology, even with a clear catalyst.
    *   **Kill Criteria:** If the validation plan yields zero sign-ups and follow-up interviews reveal a deep-seated unwillingness to switch tools regardless of the new risks.

    *   **Kill Criteria:** If a core, irreplaceable data source becomes unavailable or prohibitively expensive.
3.  **Risk: A major incumbent responds quickly.** Carrier411 or DAT quickly builds a similar "compliance log" feature, neutralizing the core wedge.
    *   **Kill Criteria:** If an incumbent ships a directly competitive feature within the first 6 months and leverages their distribution to make customer acquisition impossible.
