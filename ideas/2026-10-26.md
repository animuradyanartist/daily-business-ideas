#### MVP Cut: Inclusions and Exclusions for V1

The Minimum Viable Product (MVP) will be rigorously scoped to address the most acute NFPA 96 compliance pain points, ensuring rapid time-to-market and focused value delivery.

**Inclusions for V1:**
*   **Digital NFPA 96 Inspection Forms/Checklists:** Customizable, guided forms reflecting the latest NFPA 96 (2025 edition) requirements for commercial kitchen exhaust system inspections and cleaning. This includes sections for pre-cleaning conditions, post-cleaning verification, and deficiency identification.
*   **Photo Capture with Timestamp and Geolocation:** Mobile functionality for technicians to capture before-and-after photos, as well as images of deficiencies, automatically embedding date, time, and location data for audit-proof evidence.
*   **Automated Service Certificate Generation:** One-click generation of professional, NFPA 96-compliant service certificates that can be provided to clients, demonstrating adherence to cleaning schedules and standards.
*   **Client Portal for Report Access:** A secure, read-only web portal for clients (e.g., restaurant managers, property owners) to view their service certificates, inspection reports, and photo documentation, reducing administrative inquiries.
*   **Basic Scheduling for Recurring Cleanings:** Functionality to set up and track recurring cleaning schedules based on NFPA 96 frequency requirements (e.g., monthly, quarterly, semi-annually, annually) tied to cooking load.
*   **User Management (Technicians & Office Staff):** Role-based access control for field technicians to complete inspections and for office staff to manage schedules, generate reports, and oversee compliance.
*   **Basic Audit-Ready Reporting:** A dashboard and exportable reports summarizing compliance status, cleaning history, and identified deficiencies across all client locations, designed to satisfy fire marshal audits.

**Exclusions for V1 (and rationale):**
*   **Advanced CRM Features:** Detailed client history beyond compliance, marketing automation, or lead management. Rationale: Focus on core compliance; existing CRMs or simpler tools can handle these in the interim.
*   **Full-Fledged Invoicing/Payment Processing:** Direct billing, payment collection, or integration with payment gateways. Rationale: Integrate with existing accounting software (e.g., QuickBooks) to avoid building a complex financial module in V1.
*   **Complex Dispatch Optimization:** AI-driven route planning, real-time technician tracking, or dynamic rescheduling based on traffic. Rationale: Generic field service management (FSM) software often provides these; V1 prioritizes compliance over advanced logistics.
*   **Integration with Fire Suppression Systems (NFPA 17A):** While a logical expansion, NFPA 17A compliance is a separate standard. Rationale: Focus on NFPA 96 first to achieve market fit and avoid scope creep.
