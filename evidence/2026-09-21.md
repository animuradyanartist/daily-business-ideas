The opportunity is to create a focused software tool that automates the mandatory federal Brewer's Report of Operations (BROP) for small US craft breweries. This would save brewers time and reduce the risk of errors compared to manual spreadsheet-based methods.

- Pays: The owner or head brewer of a small craft brewery. _(inference)_
- Assessment: The owner or head brewer of a small craft brewery pays to reduce the time spent on compliance paperwork and minimize the risk of costly errors.
