**Findings**
- The head-term failures were fixed by the prompt alone: all 10 v3 drafts passed, so the repair request never fired in this run (it stays as a bounded safety net and is unit-tested only).
- The prompt pushes toward broad category terms, and the demand level is the single largest measured keyword, so broader terms now decide the demand reading for 4 of 10 ideas (v2: 0). Coverage rose; so did the risk of over-reading demand.
- Two ideas changed market between v2 and v3 (2026-05-01 India → United States, 2026-05-05 United States → India), so their coverage change is not purely a planning effect. 2026-05-05's v2 figures ("microcopy examples" 110/mo) were United States data.
- "kit" still appears in v3 keywords ("ux research kit", "freelance client kit", "case study kit", "ai prompt kit") despite the prompt; one of them ("ux research kit", 10/mo) returned data.

**Recommendation:** Keep the v3 prompt for keyword shape and coverage, but do not let a broad head term set the demand level: have the planner tag each keyword as idea-scope or category-scope and compute the demand level from idea-scope keywords, showing category volume separately. Until then, read v3 demand levels together with the second table above. The daily pipeline stays in dry run, so this affects no live reading yet; it is a blocker before live mode.
