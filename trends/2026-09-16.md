## Trend 1: AI-Automated Water Utility Compliance

**What's happening** — Small and mid-sized water utilities are adopting AI-powered platforms to automate the monitoring and reporting required for environmental and public health compliance. These tools ingest data from sensors and operational systems to continuously track water quality and generate auditable records for regulators.

**Why now** — Regulatory demands, particularly from the EPA regarding contaminants like PFAS, are increasing, while many smaller utilities lack the specialized staff to manage complex compliance manually. AI platforms are emerging to productize this expertise, offering continuous monitoring, predictive maintenance, and automated reporting. Startups like Connected Things and NatureDots are building AI-driven platforms to help utilities turn fragmented data into real-time operational and compliance insights. This allows under-resourced teams to meet state and federal requirements, such as those under the Safe Drinking Water Act, more efficiently.

**Forecast (3–6 months)** — The trend will evolve from "monitoring and reporting" to "agentic" operational management. An AI agent will connect to a utility's SCADA systems, sensor networks, and asset management logs. It will not only flag compliance risks but will autonomously take corrective actions, such as adjusting chemical dosing in a treatment plant or generating a work order for a pipe section showing signs of imminent failure. The human operator's role will shift from direct control to oversight of the AI managing the system.

**Who rides this** — A solo creator could develop "The Small Utility's AI Compliance Readiness Kit." This digital product would provide a toolkit for managers of small water systems to prepare their operations for AI. It would include Notion templates for mapping regulatory obligations to specific data sources and checklists for ensuring data quality, creating a clear implementation plan before adopting a specialized AI platform.

**Confidence:** high — This trend automates a non-discretionary, high-stakes workflow for a fragmented market of non-technical operators. The risk of massive fines and public health crises provides a powerful incentive to adopt cost-effective compliance solutions.

## Trend 2: AI-Powered Audit Readiness for SMBs

**What's happening** — Small and mid-sized businesses are adopting AI-powered tools to automate the preparation for financial audits. These platforms connect to accounting systems to continuously monitor transactions, test internal controls, and generate the evidence trail required by auditors.

**Why now** — Audit preparation is a manual, time-consuming process that distracts finance teams from their core work. AI tools are now productizing the concept of "continuous audit readiness." Platforms like FloQast and other automated reporting software use AI to automate reconciliations, analyze variances, and maintain a real-time audit trail. This shifts the process from a year-end scramble to an ongoing, automated state of preparedness, reducing the cost and stress of the audit process.

**Forecast (3–6 months)** — The trend will evolve from "readiness" to "agentic" audit management. An AI agent will be given read-only access to a company's financial systems. It will autonomously perform the work of a junior auditor, continuously testing controls, sampling transactions, and documenting evidence. When an external audit begins, the AI will provide the human auditors with a complete, pre-packaged evidence locker and answer their initial queries, significantly reducing the manual effort required from the company's finance team.

**Who rides this** — A solo creator could develop "The SMB's AI Audit Prep Checklist." This digital product would provide a toolkit for small business owners and finance managers to structure their financial workflows for AI-powered auditing. It would include Notion templates for documenting internal controls and a guide for configuring accounting software to produce the clean, structured data these AI platforms require.

**Confidence:** high — This trend automates a high-cost, high-anxiety, and recurring business process. The value proposition of reducing audit fees and freeing up senior finance staff time is compelling for any SMB.

## Trend 3: AI-Automated Creator & Brand Deal Management

**What's happening** — Brands and creators are adopting AI-powered platforms to automate the entire lifecycle of influencer marketing campaigns, from discovery and outreach to contract negotiation and performance tracking.

**Why now** — Managing creator collaborations is a high-friction, manual process involving spreadsheets, emails, and disconnected tools. As brands scale their creator programs, this becomes a major operational bottleneck. AI platforms from companies like Superdeal, Hypefy, and CreatorCatalyst are now automating this entire workflow. These "agentic" systems can take a natural language brief, source and vet creators, handle outreach and rate negotiation, manage content approvals, and report on campaign results, all within a single platform. This allows brands to run campaigns with thousands of creators without adding headcount.

**Forecast (3–6 months)** — The trend will evolve from "workflow automation" to "agentic" portfolio management for creators. A creator's AI agent will proactively scan for brand deal opportunities that match their profile and audience. It will conduct initial negotiations based on the creator's predefined rate card and availability, presenting the creator with a shortlist of fully-vetted, pre-negotiated offers to choose from. This will flip the model from creators reactively responding to inbound requests to proactively managing a pipeline of AI-sourced opportunities.

**Who rides this** — A solo creator could develop "The Creator's AI Deal-Flow Kit." This digital product would provide a toolkit for influencers to structure their personal brand and business operations for these AI platforms. It would include Notion templates for creating a comprehensive media kit, a rate card with different deliverable packages, and pre-written responses for common negotiation points that can be used to train their AI agent.

**Confidence:** high — This trend automates a complex, high-value workflow in a rapidly growing multi-billion dollar market. The value proposition of scaling marketing efforts while reducing manual overhead is extremely strong for brands of all sizes.

## Patterns I noticed today

- The most powerful B2B software opportunities often involve de-risking an individual's professional license, not just the company's finances.
- A market of many small, fragmented, non-technical buyers (like small utilities) is a feature, not a bug. It creates a natural barrier against large enterprise competitors and rewards simple, focused product design.
- Regulatory complexity that varies state-by-state can be a powerful moat. Building a business that masters this complexity creates a durable competitive advantage.
