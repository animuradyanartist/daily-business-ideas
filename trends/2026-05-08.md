## Trend 1: AI-Generated Video for Marketing

**What's happening** — Marketers and solo creators are using generative AI tools to create short-form videos from text prompts. This moves beyond repurposing existing content to generating entirely new video assets, including scenes, characters, and voiceovers, for social media ads and organic posts.

**Why now** — The technology for text-to-video has rapidly improved in quality and accessibility. Tools like Haiper, InVideo AI, and Luma Labs' Dream Machine are enabling the creation of short, branded videos in minutes, a process that previously required stock footage, editing software, and significant time. This addresses the high demand for a constant stream of video content on platforms like TikTok and Instagram Reels, allowing for rapid A/B testing of ad creatives and marketing messages.

**Forecast (3–6 months)** — AI-generated video will become the standard for producing high-volume, short-form marketing content. The competitive differentiator will shift from the base video generation to platforms offering fine-tuned control over brand assets (logos, colors, fonts) and character consistency across multiple videos. This will create a market for digital products that teach marketers how to write effective video prompts and structure narrative arcs for 15-30 second ad spots.

**Who rides this** — A solo creator with design skills can create and sell "AI Video Ad Creation Kits." These would be Notion workbooks for storyboarding short ads, paired with a library of prompts tested to produce high-quality, commercially-usable video clips from popular generative video tools.

**Confidence:** high — A large number of sources from April and May 2026 confirm the launch and rapid improvement of accessible text-to-video tools aimed directly at marketers and creators.

## Trend 2: AI-Powered Competitive Intelligence

**What's happening** — Startups and small businesses are using AI-powered platforms to automate the process of monitoring and analyzing their competitors. These tools continuously scan the web for competitor activities—like pricing changes, new feature launches, marketing campaigns, and customer reviews—and deliver synthesized intelligence reports.

**Why now** — Manual competitive analysis is time-consuming and often incomplete. A new generation of AI tools like Klue, Crayon, and Kompyte automates this data collection and synthesis. They provide real-time alerts and dashboards, turning unstructured market data into actionable insights. This allows even a solo founder to maintain a level of market awareness that was previously only achievable by dedicated analyst teams.

**Forecast (3–6 months)** — Automated competitive intelligence will become a standard operational tool for founders and marketers. The market will mature from simple alert systems to predictive platforms that can forecast a competitor's next move based on hiring patterns or shifts in marketing language. This creates an opportunity for digital products that teach founders how to interpret these signals and build a "response playbook" for common competitive threats.

**Who rides this** — A solo creator can build and sell "The Solo Founder's Competitive Intelligence Dashboard." This could be a Notion template pre-configured to track key competitor metrics, paired with a guide on how to feed data from AI monitoring tools into the dashboard to make strategic decisions.

**Confidence:** high — Multiple sources from early 2026 confirm the growth of AI-powered competitive intelligence platforms as a distinct category, solving the critical business need for scalable market awareness.

## Trend 3: AI-Powered Personal Financial Planning

**What's happening** — Consumers are adopting AI-powered apps to get personalized financial advice and automated money management. These tools go beyond simple budgeting by analyzing a user's spending, income, and financial goals to provide specific, actionable recommendations on saving, investing, and debt repayment.

**Why now** — Traditional financial advice is expensive and inaccessible for many. AI has reached a point where it can provide sophisticated, personalized financial planning at a fraction of the cost. Apps like Monarch Money and Copilot are using AI to categorize transactions, forecast cash flow, and create custom budgets automatically. This provides users with a level of financial oversight previously reserved for high-net-worth individuals.

**Forecast (3–6 months)** — AI financial planners will become mainstream, moving from budgeting tools to proactive financial agents that can negotiate bills, optimize savings, and execute investment strategies on the user's behalf. The key differentiator will be the level of trust and the AI's ability to explain its recommendations clearly. This will create a market for educational products that help users understand the financial principles behind the AI's advice, enabling them to use the tools more effectively.

**Who rides this** — A creator can develop digital products for users of these new AI finance apps. An example is "The Financial Literacy Kit for the AI Era," a set of guides and calculators (in Notion or Google Sheets) that explains concepts like asset allocation and tax optimization, helping users set better goals for their AI planner to execute.

**Confidence:** high — A significant number of recent sources highlight the rapid growth and increasing sophistication of AI-native personal finance applications, positioning them as direct competitors to both traditional banks and human financial advisors.

## Patterns I noticed today

-   AI is enabling a new class of "intelligence" products that automate the work of a human analyst, whether it's for competitive intelligence, financial planning, or market research.
-   The primary business opportunity is shifting from just providing the AI tool to providing the "scaffolding" around it—the educational content, templates, and workflows that teach people how to use the tool to achieve a specific business or personal outcome.
-   For every high-cost professional service (video production, market analysis, financial advice), an AI-powered alternative is emerging that is 10x cheaper and faster, creating entirely new markets of users who were previously priced out.
