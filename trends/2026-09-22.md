## Trend 1: AI-Automated Insurance Claim Documentation for Contractors

**What's happening** — Restoration and roofing contractors are adopting AI platforms to automate the creation of detailed damage estimates and documentation required for insurance claims. These tools use computer vision to analyze photos of property damage and generate structured reports that align with insurance carrier requirements.

**Why now** — The claims process is an adversarial, high-friction workflow where contractors must provide extensive evidence to justify their repair estimates to insurance adjusters. AI is now being used to standardize this evidence. Tools can analyze photos to identify damage types, measure quantities, and compare a contractor's estimate against the insurer's, highlighting discrepancies. This is happening as insurers themselves are rapidly adopting AI for claims intake and damage assessment, creating a need for contractors to submit data in a machine-readable, defensible format.

**Forecast (3–6 months)** — The trend will evolve from "damage documentation" to "agentic claim negotiation." A contractor's AI agent will ingest photos and project scope, then generate and submit the full claim package to the insurance carrier's AI system. The two agents will autonomously negotiate line items, with the contractor's agent referencing historical claim data, local material costs, and building codes to justify the estimate. The system will flag only the items that require human intervention, turning the multi-week negotiation process into a largely automated, data-driven exchange.

**Who rides this** — A solo creator could develop "The Contractor's AI-Ready Insurance Claim Kit." This digital product would provide a toolkit for small contracting businesses to structure their on-site documentation process. It would include Notion templates for capturing job site data, checklists for photo and video evidence, and pre-written communication templates for submitting the AI-generated reports to adjusters with clear, professional language.

**Confidence:** high — This trend automates a critical, revenue-gating workflow for a massive market of non-technical SMBs who are in a structurally adversarial relationship with large, powerful insurance carriers. The ROI is direct and immediate.

## Trend 2: AI-Personalized Pet Nutrition & Wellness Plans

**What's happening** — A new category of consumer apps is using AI to generate personalized nutrition and wellness plans for pets. These tools analyze a pet's specific data—breed, age, weight, activity level, and allergies—to create custom meal plans and proactive health guidance, moving beyond generic advice.

**Why now** — Pet owners are increasingly seeking personalized care for their animals but find generic feeding guides on packaging inadequate. In response, AI-powered apps like FAMMO and PetCare AI are emerging to provide data-driven nutrition plans. These platforms calculate precise portion sizes and nutrient requirements, recommend specific foods, and track health metrics over time. A recent survey found that 55% of pet owners are now using AI for nutrition advice, indicating a rapid shift in consumer behavior toward trusting algorithmic recommendations for pet care.

**Forecast (3–6 months)** — The trend will evolve from "plan generation" to "agentic wellness management." A pet owner's AI agent will connect to their pet's wearable data (like from a FitBark or Whistle collar), their electronic vet records, and the owner's online grocery account. The agent will autonomously monitor the pet's activity and health data, adjust their nutrition plan in real-time, and automatically add the correct food and supplements to the owner's weekly shopping cart.

**Who rides this** — A solo creator could develop "The New Pet Parent's AI Wellness Kit." This digital product would be a toolkit for first-time dog or cat owners. It would include a Notion-based "Pet Handbook" for consolidating all of a pet's information (vet records, vaccination dates, feeding schedules) in one place, making it easy to input into an AI nutrition app. It could also include a guide to interpreting the AI's recommendations and questions to ask a veterinarian.

**Confidence:** high — This trend addresses a high-anxiety, emotionally-driven need for a massive consumer market with a proven willingness to spend on pets. It avoids the regulatory complexity of human health while tapping into the same desire for personalized, proactive care.

## Trend 3: AI-Automated Code Review for Small Teams

**What's happening** — Software development teams are adopting AI-powered tools that automatically review code changes in pull requests (PRs). These tools act as an AI "teammate," providing line-by-line feedback on bugs, security risks, and style issues, reducing the manual workload on human reviewers.

**Why now** — The proliferation of AI coding assistants like GitHub Copilot has dramatically increased the volume of code being written, creating a review bottleneck. Human review capacity has not kept pace, leading to slower development cycles. In response, a new class of AI code review tools from companies like CodeRabbit, CodeAnt AI, and Sonar has emerged. These tools integrate directly into platforms like GitHub and provide instant, automated feedback on every PR, summarizing changes and flagging potential issues before a human reviewer needs to look at them.

**Forecast (3–6 months)** — The trend will evolve from "pull request review" to "agentic" software development. A developer will assign a task or issue to an AI agent. The agent will then autonomously write the code, create a pull request, review its own work against project standards, respond to feedback from human teammates, and merge the code once it passes all checks. The developer's role will shift from writing code to defining tasks and governing the AI agent's work.

**Who rides this** — A solo creator could develop "The Solopreneur Dev's AI Quality Checklist." This digital product would be a toolkit for freelance developers and small teams. It would provide a Notion template for creating a "project constitution"—a structured document defining a project's specific coding conventions, architectural patterns, and testing requirements. This document could then be used as a context file to configure an AI code review tool for more accurate and project-specific feedback.

**Confidence:** high — This trend addresses a critical bottleneck in a core workflow for a massive, high-value professional market. The ROI in terms of increased development velocity and improved code quality is direct and substantial.

## Status update: AI as a Thought Partner

**Originally flagged:** 2026-04-29

**Current status:** 🚀 accelerating

**What changed** — The concept of using AI as a "thought partner" or "sparring partner" is solidifying as a core use case for professionals. The focus is shifting from simple brainstorming to a more rigorous process of pressure-testing ideas and uncovering blind spots. Recent articles and guides explicitly frame AI's role not as a replacement for human creativity, but as a tool to challenge assumptions and add nuance. This pattern is appearing in multiple professional contexts, from lawyers using AI to identify new perspectives in cases to corporate teams using AI facilitators in strategy workshops to challenge groupthink.
