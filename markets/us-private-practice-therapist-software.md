# Market: US Private Practice Therapist Software
slug: us-private-practice-therapist-software

## 2026-09-07 revision — idea: The Private Practice Therapist's "Audit-Proof" Note Generator (score: 99)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM: $8.4B+ Global Mental Health Software Market. SAM: 150,000 US insurance-accepting therapists × $420 ACV = $63M. SOM (Y2): 1,500 therapists = $630k ARR.
- Growth: Market growing at 17-21% CAGR, driven by rising mental health needs and digital adoption.
- Fragmentation: Highly fragmented buyer side, dominated by thousands of solo and small group practices. Ideal for PLG.
- Margin pool: Margin sits with software vendors who can solve high-stakes administrative problems (billing, compliance, documentation) that directly impact a therapist's revenue and liability.
- Distribution control: Incumbent EHRs (SimplePractice, TherapyNotes) own the core practice management workflow, but are vulnerable to best-in-class point solutions for specific pains like documentation.
- Incumbents and inertia: Incumbents are all-in-one practice management suites. Their note-taking features are often basic text boxes. This structural focus on "management" over "clinical workflow" leaves a gap for a superior, AI-native note-writing assistant.
- Regulatory / platform risk: HIPAA compliance is a critical, non-negotiable requirement. A data breach would be an extinction-level event.
