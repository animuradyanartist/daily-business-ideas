# Market: Independent Land Surveyor Software (US)
slug: independent-land-surveyor-software-us

## 2026-07-12 revision — idea: The independent land surveyor's "deed plot" & QA hub (score: 96)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM: ~$11.5B US Surveying & Mapping market. SAM: ~10,000 independent firms x $2,388/yr = $23.8M. SOM (Yr 3): 250 firms x $2,388/yr = ~$600k ARR.
- Growth: US market growing at a CAGR of ~8.11%. Driven by construction, real estate, and infrastructure development.
- Fragmentation: Highly fragmented. ~80% of the 12,915-17,511 US firms are single-owner operations.
- Margin pool: Margin sits with the licensed professional's services. They buy software to increase efficiency and, critically, to reduce professional liability risk.
- Distribution control: Decentralized. Access via professional associations (NSPS), online communities, and content marketing demonstrating expertise and accuracy.
- Incumbents and inertia: Incumbent Autodesk (Civil 3D) provides a complex, expensive, all-in-one platform. Inertia is strong due to manual workflows being "good enough." The gap is for a specialized, fast, and highly accurate tool that solves one critical, high-risk task better than the incumbent.
- Regulatory / platform risk: Low. Not dependent on a single platform. Must maintain high accuracy to avoid liability.
