# Market: US Small Fleet IFTA Compliance Software
slug: us-small-fleet-ifta-compliance-software

## 2026-09-24 revision — idea: The Small Trucking Fleet's "Audit-Ready" IFTA Fuel Tax Hub (score: 92)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: ~$500M TAM (IFTA software market) → $159M SAM (~530k small fleets @ $300/yr) → $80k SOM (Year 1 goal). Sources: Guideflow, ATA.
- Growth: IFTA software market growing at 10.2% CAGR. Source: Verified Market Reports via Guideflow.
- Fragmentation: Extremely high. 91.5% of ~580,000 interstate carriers operate 10 or fewer trucks. Source: ATA.
- Margin pool: Sits with the software provider who can automate the complex, recurring, high-stakes compliance task with the best user experience.
- Distribution control: None. Customers are reachable directly via online communities and content marketing.
- Incumbents and inertia: Incumbents are either expensive/complex ELD suites (Motive, Samsara) or cheap/manual point solutions (ExpressIFTA). This leaves a clear gap for an affordable, automated tool designed for small fleets.
- Regulatory / platform risk: The IFTA regulation is deeply entrenched and unlikely to change. The primary risk is reliance on ELD provider APIs for data automation.
