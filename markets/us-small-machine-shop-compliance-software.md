# Market: US Small Machine Shop Compliance Software
slug: us-small-machine-shop-compliance-software

## 2026-09-08 revision — idea: The Small Machine Shop's "Audit-Ready" Material Traceability Log (score: 100)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM: $46.3B US Machine Shop market. SAM: ~4,275 shops needing AS9100 compliance × $3k/yr = ~$12.8M ARR. SOM (Y2): 85 shops × $3k/yr = $255k ARR.
- Growth: Strong. US metalworking machinery orders hit a record $3.44B in H1 2026, up 36% YoY, driven by aerospace.
- Fragmentation: Highly fragmented. ~17,100 shops in the US, most with <15 employees.
- Margin pool: Sits with shops that can win and retain high-value, high-compliance (e.g., AS9100) contracts from prime aerospace and defense contractors.
- Distribution control: Direct access to buyers via industry forums (Practical Machinist), content marketing, and paid search targeting compliance-related keywords.
- Incumbents and inertia: Incumbents are high-cost, high-complexity ERPs (ProShop, etc.). Their inertia and high-touch sales model leave a gap for a low-cost, self-serve point solution to capture the long tail of smaller shops.
- Regulatory / platform risk: Low. The primary driver is customer and regulatory compliance (AS9100), which is a tailwind. These requirements are foundational to safety and are unlikely to be relaxed.
