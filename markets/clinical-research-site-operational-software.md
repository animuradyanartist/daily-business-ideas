# Market: Clinical Research Site Operational Software (US)
slug: clinical-research-site-operational-software

## 2026-06-14 revision — idea: The Clinical Trial Site's "Patient Stipend & Compliance" Hub (score: 93)
- **TAM/SAM/SOM:** TAM: ~$918M (North America clinical trial payments market). SAM: ~$37.5M (15k independent sites x $2.5k/yr). SOM (Y2): ~$187.5k.
- **Growth:** 9.6% CAGR, driven by increasing trial volume, complexity, and focus on patient retention.
- **Fragmentation:** Highly fragmented buyer side with thousands of independent research sites, often underserved by enterprise software.
- **Margin pool:** Sits with software vendors who can reduce high-cost manual labor (CRC time) and mitigate catastrophic financial risk (patient dropout).
- **Distribution control:** Incumbents (Greenphire, IQVIA) have deep channels into large sponsors and CROs. The opportunity is to reach the long tail of independent sites directly via content marketing and targeted outreach.
- **Incumbents and inertia:** Incumbents sell complex, expensive, all-in-one systems focused on payment disbursement. They leave an opening for a simple, affordable, tracking-focused tool for smaller sites that can't afford or don't need the full enterprise suite.
- **Regulatory / platform risk:** High regulatory environment (FDA, IRS reporting) creates a moat. A product must have compliance built-in. No major platform risk.
