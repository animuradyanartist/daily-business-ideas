# Market: US SMB I-9 Compliance Software
slug: us-smb-i9-compliance-software

## 2026-08-18 revision — idea: The HR Manager's "I-9 Audit-Ready" Document Collector (score: 98)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM: 6M+ US employers. SAM: ~653k firms (20-499 employees) x ~$1k ACV = ~$652M/yr. SOM (Yr 2): 1,000 firms x $1k ACV = $1M ARR.
- Growth: Stable market with growth driven by new business formation and regulatory changes that increase the perceived risk of non-compliance.
- Fragmentation: Buyer base (SMBs) is highly fragmented. Supplier side has a few specialized players and bundled features within large HRIS platforms.
- Margin pool: Sits with the software provider, who captures value by mitigating significant financial risk (fines) for a relatively low monthly fee.
- Distribution control: No single entity controls distribution. Reachable via content marketing, SEO, paid search, and communities like SHRM.
- Incumbents and inertia: Large HRIS providers (ADP, Workday) have bundled I-9 features but are not best-in-class. Enterprise solutions (Tracker I-9) are too expensive for SMBs. This leaves a gap for a focused, affordable solution.
- Regulatory / platform risk: The entire market is created by USCIS/ICE regulation. A simplification of the Form I-9 or a change in enforcement policy could impact the market.
- Key sources: https://www.ice.gov/features/i9-inspection, https://www.lawlogix.com/blog/ice-just-made-i-9-compliance-a-lot-harder/, https://www.i9intelligence.com/pricing/
