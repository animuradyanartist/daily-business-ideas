# Market: US Independent Self-Storage Lien Compliance Software
slug: us-independent-self-storage-lien-compliance-software

## 2026-08-24 revision — idea: The Self-Storage Operator's Delinquent Unit & Lien Sale Automator (score: 99)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM: $3.24B (Global Self-Storage Software 2026). SAM: 32,500 independent US facilities × $1,200/yr = $39M. SOM: 130 facilities x $1,200/yr = $156k.
- Growth: Market growing at 5-6% CAGR, software component at 12%+.
- Fragmentation: Buyer base is highly fragmented; over 65% of 50,000+ US facilities are independent operators.
- Margin pool: Sits with software and service providers who can reduce catastrophic legal risk for operators.
- Distribution control: Incumbent FMS providers (SiteLink, storEDGE) have existing customer relationships. New entrants must use content marketing (SEO) and industry communities.
- Incumbents and inertia: Incumbents offer this as a generic feature in a complex, all-in-one platform. They are not focused on providing a best-in-class, state-by-state, legally-audited compliance workflow.
- Regulatory / platform risk: High. The entire product is based on correctly interpreting and maintaining 50 different sets of state laws. A mistake carries significant liability.
