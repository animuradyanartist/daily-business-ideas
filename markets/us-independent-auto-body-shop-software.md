# Market: US Independent Auto Body Shop Software
slug: us-independent-auto-body-shop-software

## 2026-08-04 revision — idea: The independent auto body shop's "get paid by insurance" supplement evidence locker (score: 98)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM: $3.4B (Global auto repair software). SAM: $104.3M (43,685 US independent shops x $2,388/yr). SOM: $520k (0.5% of SAM).
- Growth: Market is mature, but repair complexity and cost are rising, increasing the need for software to manage operations and profitability.
- Fragmentation: The buyer side (independent shops) is highly fragmented, with the majority being family-owned businesses. The payor side (insurance) is highly concentrated.
- Margin pool: Margin sits with the body shops but is under constant pressure from insurers. This tool helps defend and recover that margin.
- Distribution control: No single entity controls distribution. Access is via open digital channels, industry media, and communities.
- Incumbents and inertia: Incumbents are all-in-one Shop Management Systems. They leave this gap open because their documentation features are general-purpose, not optimized for the specific, adversarial workflow of winning a supplement dispute.
- Regulatory / platform risk: Low. No significant regulatory or platform risk.
