slug: childcare-center-compliance-software-us

## 2026-06-22 revision — idea: The Childcare Center's "Stay-in-Ratio" Staffing & Compliance Dashboard (score: 96)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM: ~$467.8M (519k businesses x $900/yr). SAM: ~$280.7M (targeting 60% independent centers). SOM (Yr 3): ~$1.4M.
- Growth: Childcare market growing at 4-8% annually. Childcare software sub-market growing at ~8.7% annually.
- Fragmentation: Highly fragmented market of independent small business owners. Ideal for self-serve SaaS.
- Margin pool: Software has high gross margins (>90%). Buyers (childcare centers) have very low net margins (1-5%), making them price-sensitive but also highly motivated to buy tools that prevent catastrophic costs (fines, closure).
- Distribution control: No single channel dominates. Controlled by a mix of SEO, content marketing, and community engagement in professional groups.
- Incumbents and inertia: Incumbents (Procare, Brightwheel) are large, all-in-one platforms. Their complexity and high/opaque pricing create a gap for a simple, affordable point solution for the most acute compliance pain.
- Regulatory / platform risk: Low. The business is built on top of government regulation, which is a stable foundation. No platform risk.
