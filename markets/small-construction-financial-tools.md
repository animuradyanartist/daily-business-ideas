# Market: Small Construction Contractors (Financial Tools)
slug: small-construction-financial-tools

## 2026-06-03 revision — idea: The Small Construction Co's "Know Your Number" Job Costing Kit (score: 82)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM: 3.2M US firms. SAM: 2.6M firms <10 employees. SOM (Yr 3): 2.6k firms x $999 = $2.6M ARR. (Sources: Zippia, Statista)
- Growth: US construction market growing at 4.4% CAGR. Key driver is not growth but persistent pressure on thin (1.4-2.4%) profit margins. (Sources: Statista, ForConstructionPros)
- Fragmentation: Extremely high. 99.94% of firms are small businesses, 81.5% have <10 employees. No single channel reaches them.
- Margin pool: Sits with the contractors who can accurately price jobs and control costs. Inaccurate costing erodes 5-10% of margin.
- Distribution control: Fragmented. Incumbents like Intuit (QuickBooks) have massive reach but a generic product that fails at the specific pain.
- Incumbents and inertia: QuickBooks is the default but inadequate. Procore/Buildertrend are too complex/expensive. The gap between them is the opportunity.
- Regulatory / platform risk: Low. Standard B2B software. No platform dependency.
