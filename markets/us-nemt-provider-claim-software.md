# Market: US NEMT Provider Claim & Billing Software
slug: us-nemt-provider-claim-software

## 2026-08-08 revision — idea: The NEMT provider's "get paid by Medicaid" claim preparer (score: 98)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM: $12.5B (US NEMT Market Spend 2026). SAM: $10.7M (6,000 small/mid-size providers x $1,788 ACV). SOM (Y2): $107k (60 customers).
- Growth: Market growing at 5.5-7.5% CAGR, driven by aging population and Medicaid expansion.
- Fragmentation: Highly fragmented. ~10,000 providers in the US, with no single company holding more than 5% market share.
- Margin pool: NEMT operators run on thin 8-15% net margins for Medicaid trips, making cash flow acceleration a critical need. The software margin is high (>90%).
- Distribution control: Customer access is fragmented across industry-specific Facebook groups, blogs, and direct outreach. No single channel dominates.
- Incumbents and inertia: Incumbent software vendors are focused on dispatch and operations. Their billing modules are often add-ons, not core competencies, leaving a gap for a best-in-class financial compliance tool.
- Regulatory / platform risk: High. Medicaid billing rules are complex, vary by state, and change frequently. This is both a risk and the source of the moat.
