# Market: US Non-Emergency Medical Transportation (NEMT) Provider Software
slug: us-nemt-provider-software

## 2026-09-19 revision — idea: The Non-Emergency Medical Transportation (NEMT) Provider's "Get Paid, Not Buried" Hub (score: 100)
*   **TAM/SAM/SOM:** TAM: $15.5B by 2032 (NEMT service market). SAM: ~$12.6M annually (3,500 small providers x $3,600/yr). SOM: $90k ARR in Year 1.
*   **Growth:** The underlying NEMT market is growing at 8.5% CAGR, driven by an aging population.
*   **Fragmentation:** Highly fragmented market of thousands of small, independent providers. Ideal for a product-led or light-touch sales approach.
*   **Margin pool:** Margin sits with the software provider who can successfully abstract away the complexity of state-by-state Medicaid billing and claim submission.
*   **Distribution control:** No single entity controls distribution. Providers can be reached directly via online marketing, content, and industry associations.
*   **Incumbents and inertia:** Incumbents like RouteGenie and TripMaster exist but are often complex and target larger fleets, leaving an opening for a simpler, more affordable solution focused on the small operator's core "get paid" problem.
*   **Regulatory / platform risk:** High. The business is entirely dependent on state-level Medicaid rules and submission platforms. A change in regulations could require significant product rework.
