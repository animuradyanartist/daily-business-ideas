- Growth: Market growing at 8.6% CAGR. Pain is accelerating due to a 60% YoY increase in strategic fraud.
- Fragmentation: Highly fragmented buyer base with over 26,000 licensed brokers, most of them small businesses. No concentration of buying power.
- Margin pool: Margin sits with the software provider that can successfully mitigate catastrophic, uninsurable risk for a flat fee that is a fraction of the potential loss or the cost of manual labor.

- Incumbents and inertia: Incumbents are legacy data monitoring services. They are vulnerable to a modern workflow tool built to address the new realities of sophisticated fraud and heightened legal liability.
- Regulatory / platform risk: A May 2026 Supreme Court ruling is a primary driver of the opportunity. Risk is that a core data source (e.g., FMCSA API) could become restricted.
