## 2026-07-27 revision — idea: The veterinary clinic's "DEA audit-ready" controlled substance log (score: 98)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM: $37.4B US Vet Services Market. SAM: ~40,000 clinics x $1,188/yr = $47.5M. SOM (Yr 3): 600 clinics x $1,188/yr = ~$712k ARR. (Sources: Fortune Business Insights, AVMA)
- Growth: Vet services market growing at 8.1% annually. (Source: Fortune Business Insights)
- Fragmentation: Highly fragmented market of mostly small, independent clinics, though corporate consolidation is a growing trend.
- Margin pool: Clinics have tight margins and are highly sensitive to unbudgeted, catastrophic fines from regulators. The value is in risk mitigation.
- Distribution control: Distribution is influenced by PIMS platforms (via integrations/marketplaces) and industry associations/publications.
- Incumbents and inertia: Large PIMS platforms have basic features but are not focused on this specific, high-stakes workflow, leaving a gap for a best-in-class point solution.
- Regulatory / platform risk: The primary driver is the DEA; a change in their enforcement posture could alter the market. Risk of PIMS platforms improving their modules is moderate.
