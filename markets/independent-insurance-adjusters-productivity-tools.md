# Market: Independent Insurance Adjusters' Productivity Tools
slug: independent-insurance-adjusters-productivity-tools

## 2026-06-08 revision — idea: The Independent Insurance Adjuster's "Claim Narrative" AI Assistant (score: 88)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM: >125k US claims professionals. SAM: ~37.5k independent adjusters. SOM (Y3): 1,125 adjusters ($561k ARR). Method: 30% of total professionals are independent, targeting 3% penetration. Sources: [propertycasualty360.com](https://www.propertycasualty360.com/2017/02/15/independent-adjusters-handle-billions-in-claims/), [naia.com](https://www.naia.com/about-independent-claims-adjusters).
- Growth: Market for adjusters is projected to slightly decline, but complexity of claims and pressure for efficiency are increasing, driving demand for productivity tools. Source: [clmclaims.com](https://www.clmclaims.com/magazine/articles/five-challenges-facing-claims-adjusters-today).
- Fragmentation: Highly fragmented. No single adjusting firm has >5% market share. Buyers are individuals. Source: [ibisworld.com](https://www.ibisworld.com/united-states/market-research-reports/claims-adjusting-industry/).
- Margin pool: Sits with the individual adjuster. Their personal efficiency directly determines their income. Any tool that increases claim throughput has a clear, immediate ROI.
- Distribution control: Decentralized. Access through online communities, industry associations, and rosters of hundreds of small-to-medium IA firms. No single gatekeeper.
- Incumbents and inertia: Incumbents are carrier-focused enterprise software (clunky, not built for solo users) or horizontal AI tools (lack specific workflows and templates). Inertia is the manual use of Word/Excel templates. The gap is a purpose-built, AI-native workflow tool for the solo professional.
- Regulatory / platform risk: Low. The tool assists with documentation, it does not make coverage decisions or give advice.
- Key sources: https://www.catadjuster.org/, https://www.adjusterpro.com/blog/how-much-money-does-an-insurance-adjuster-make/, https://www.verisk.com/insurance/products/xactimate/, https://www.reddit.com/r/adjusters/
