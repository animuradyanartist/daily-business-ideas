# Market: US SMB General Contractor Compliance Software
slug: us-smb-general-contractor-compliance-software

## 2026-07-20 revision — idea: The General Contractor's "lien waiver" collection & tracking hub (score: 97)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM: $2.2T (US Construction Market). SAM: $88.2M (150k GCs x $588/yr). SOM: $176k (300 GCs x $588/yr). Sources: IBISWorld, BLS.
- Growth: Stable growth, but current pressure from inflation and labor shortages increases demand for cost-control and risk-mitigation tools. Source: AGC.
- Fragmentation: Extremely high. The vast majority of the 919,000+ construction firms have fewer than 10 employees. No buyer concentration. Source: BLS.
- Margin pool: Margin sits with the General Contractor, who is responsible for project delivery and financial risk. They directly feel the pain of liens and payment issues.
- Distribution control: No single entity controls distribution. Access is through fragmented channels: trade associations, content marketing, and integrations with accounting/PM software.
- Incumbents and inertia: Incumbents like Procore and Autodesk are focused on complex, expensive, all-in-one platforms for large GCs. This leaves a massive gap for simple, affordable, single-purpose tools for the SMB segment.
- Regulatory / platform risk: Low. Lien laws are state-by-state and stable. No platform risk.
