# Market: US Personal Injury Law Firm Workflow Software
slug: us-personal-injury-law-firm-workflow-software

## 2026-09-17 revision — idea: The Personal Injury Law Firm's Medical Records Request & Tracking Hub (score: 100)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM: $73.3B Global Legal Tech Market. SAM: 30,000 US PI firms × $3,000 ACV = $90M. SOM (Y2): 250 firms = $750k ARR.
- Growth: US Legal Tech market growing at ~8% CAGR. Law firm tech spending grew 9.7% in 2025, outpacing inflation 3x.
- Fragmentation: Highly fragmented. 122,809 PI firms in the US. No single firm has >5% market share. Ideal for product-led growth.
- Margin pool: Margin sits with software that creates operational leverage, reducing reliance on non-billable paralegal hours or expensive, variable-cost outsourced services.
- Distribution control: Distribution is controlled by access to paralegal and attorney communities via content marketing (SEO), paid ads (LinkedIn), and integrations with dominant practice management systems (Clio, Filevine).
- Incumbents and inertia: Incumbents are horizontal practice management systems that don't solve this specific external workflow, or are manual service companies that don't scale well. Inertia is the status quo of using paralegals, but rising operational costs make this increasingly untenable.
- Regulatory / platform risk: HIPAA compliance is a major regulatory requirement, acting as a barrier to entry. This is a feature, not a bug.
