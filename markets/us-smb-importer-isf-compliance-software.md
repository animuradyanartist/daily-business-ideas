# Market: US SMB Importer ISF Compliance Software
slug: us-smb-importer-isf-compliance-software

## 2026-07-19 revision — idea: The US importer's "ISF (10+2) filing" assistant (score: 98)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM: >28M annual containerized imports. SAM: >$500M annual spend on ISF filing services at ~$50/filing. SOM: ~$50M segment of frequent SMB importers addressable by a self-serve tool.
- Growth: U.S. import values and volumes are growing, with July 2026 forecast to be a record month.
- Fragmentation: Buyer base is highly fragmented (thousands of SMBs). The incumbent solution is a fragmented service industry (customs brokers).
- Margin pool: Margin sits with the party that can efficiently ensure compliance. Brokers take a high margin on a simple data entry task. SaaS can capture this by automating it.
- Distribution control: Currently controlled by brokers who have the client relationship and the specialized software. A direct-to-importer play via performance marketing and content is the path to disrupt this.
- Incumbents and inertia: Brokers are incentivized to maintain the per-transaction service model. Enterprise software is too complex and expensive for SMBs. This leaves a clear gap for a simple, self-serve SaaS product.
- Regulatory / platform risk: High. The business relies on access to CBP's ACE system and is subject to their rules. The certification process is a barrier to entry and a risk.
