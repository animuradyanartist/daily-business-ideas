# Market: US Craft Brewery Compliance Software
slug: us-craft-brewery-compliance-software

## 2026-09-21 revision — idea: The Small Brewery's "TTB-Ready" Report of Operations Generator (score: 95)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM: Global craft beer market ($100B+). SAM: ~8,000 small US breweries × $2,388/yr = ~$19.1M ARR. SOM (Yr 3): 200 breweries x $2,388/yr = ~$478k ARR.
- Growth: Contracting. The number of operating breweries declined 1.8% from June 2025 to June 2026. Production volume is also down. This pressure increases the need for efficiency tools.
- Fragmentation: Highly fragmented buyer base of 9,344 breweries across 50 states.
- Margin pool: The value sits in de-risking the business from catastrophic TTB penalties/shutdown and saving 4-8 hours of a key person's time per reporting cycle.
- Distribution control: No single channel controls access. Customer acquisition is via online communities (Reddit), SEO, and industry groups (Brewers Association).
- Incumbents and inertia: Incumbents are large, complex ERPs (Ekos, Beer30) that are often too expensive and bloated for the smallest breweries, creating an "unbundling" opportunity for a focused reporting tool.
- Regulatory / platform risk: The business depends on the TTB continuing to require the Brewer's Report of Operations. This risk is low.
