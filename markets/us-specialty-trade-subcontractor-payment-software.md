# Market: US Specialty Trade Subcontractor Payment Software
slug: us-specialty-trade-subcontractor-payment-software

## 2026-08-10 revision — idea: The subcontractor's "rejection-proof" pay application generator (score: 98)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM: $2.1T+ US construction industry spend. SAM: ~200,000 professionalized subcontractors x $2,388/yr = $477.6M. SOM: Initial 0.1% capture = $477k ARR.
- Growth: US construction market projected for modest growth of ~0.6-1.6% in 2026.
- Fragmentation: Buyer side (subcontractors) is extremely fragmented (~814k employer firms), while the payer side (GCs) is more concentrated. This is ideal for a self-service SaaS model.
- Margin pool: Margin is trapped in the subcontractor's cash conversion cycle. Slow payments cost the industry $280B annually. Software that unlocks this margin for the sub has a clear value prop.
- Distribution control: Incumbents control distribution by selling top-down to GCs. The opportunity is to bypass this and go directly to the fragmented base of subcontractors via digital channels.
- Incumbents and inertia: Incumbents (Procore, Oracle) are GC-centric. Their business model and product architecture are built around the GC's needs. Building a sub-first tool would conflict with their core business, leaving a significant gap.
- Regulatory / platform risk: Low. Prompt payment laws are generally favorable to this model. Core workflow relies on industry standards like AIA forms, not a volatile platform.
