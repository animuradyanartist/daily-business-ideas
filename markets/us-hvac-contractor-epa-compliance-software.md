## 2026-07-22 revision — idea: The HVAC contractor's "EPA AIM Act-Ready" compliance log (score: 98)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM: $152B US HVAC Services Market. SAM: 40,000 commercial/mixed contractors × $708/year = $28.3M. SOM (Year 3): 800 contractors × $708/year = ~$566k ARR. (Sources: IBISWorld, Orbital)
- Growth: Market growing at 7.4% annually, but demand for this specific solution is driven by a regulatory step-change (Jan 1, 2026 AIM Act rule). (Source: Workyard, EPA)
- Fragmentation: Highly fragmented. 120k-170k businesses, 70% with <10 employees. No single buyer has pricing power. (Source: Orbital, IBISWorld)
- Margin pool: Contractors have thin net margins (5-12%) and are highly sensitive to catastrophic fines (up to $44k+/day), making them ideal buyers for a low-cost "insurance" product. (Source: contractorselling.com, EPA)
- Distribution control: Fragmented. Access via trade associations (ACCA), digital ads (Google), content marketing, and partnerships with supply houses and FSM software vendors.
- Incumbents and inertia: Enterprise incumbents (Trakref) are too expensive/complex for SMBs. Horizontal FSM platforms (ServiceTitan) are not compliance-focused. This leaves a clear gap for a purpose-built, simple SMB solution.
- Regulatory / platform risk: The opportunity is created by EPA regulation. A future loosening of rules is a risk, but the current trend is toward stricter enforcement.
