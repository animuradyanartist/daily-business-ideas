# Market: Veterinary Practice Compliance Software (US)
slug: veterinary-practice-compliance-software

## 2026-06-15 revision — idea: The Veterinary Clinic's "Audit-Proof" DEA Compliance Log (score: 94)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM: $74.5B (US Vet Services Market). SAM: $50.06M (28,000 clinics x $1,788/yr). SOM (Yr 2): $500k (280 clinics).
- Growth: Market growing at ~7.8% CAGR. Regulatory pressure from the DEA is increasing.
- Fragmentation: Highly fragmented. Over 30,000 practices, mostly small and independent. Low market share concentration.
- Margin pool: Sits with software vendors who can solve a high-stakes, non-negotiable compliance problem, allowing them to anchor value to catastrophic failure costs (six-figure fines) rather than time savings.
- Distribution control: No single gatekeeper. Access is direct to clinics via content marketing, targeted digital ads in industry publications (e.g., DVM360), and presence at industry conferences.
- Incumbents and inertia: Incumbents are large, all-in-one Practice Information Management Systems (PIMS). Their inertia comes from being the central OS of the clinic. They leave this gap open because their focus is broad (scheduling, billing, records), and they lack the specialized, audit-proof workflow for this specific compliance task.
- Regulatory / platform risk: Low. The business *is* the solution to regulation. Changes in DEA rules would be an opportunity to update the software and reinforce its value.
