# Market: US SBIR/STTR Grant Compliance Software
slug: us-sbir-sttr-grant-compliance-software

## 2026-09-13 revision — idea: The Small R&D Firm's "Audit-Ready" SBIR/STTR Grant Compliance Ledger (score: 100)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM: ~$4B+ in annual grant awards creating a multi-hundred-million dollar compliance services market. SAM: ~10,500 active awardees needing compliance, representing a ~$31.5M ARR software opportunity. SOM (Y2): ~105 customers for ~$315k ARR.
- Growth: Stable. The SBIR program is consistently funded and was reauthorized through 2025, ensuring a steady stream of new customers.
- Fragmentation: The customer base is highly fragmented, consisting of thousands of small, independent R&D businesses across all 50 states. This makes a self-serve, product-led approach effective.
- Margin pool: Sits with specialized consultants and accountants who charge high hourly rates for manual compliance work. Opportunity is to capture this margin with software.
- Distribution control: No one controls distribution. The customer list (awardee database) is public information, creating a uniquely level playing field for new entrants with a direct outreach strategy.
- Incumbents and inertia: Incumbents are high-cost service firms (Eva Garland, BBCetc) or complex ERPs (Deltek). They leave a massive gap for an affordable, simple, self-serve tool targeted at the first-time Phase I awardee.
- Regulatory / platform risk: Low. The compliance rules are set by the federal government and change slowly. The business does not depend on a single platform.
