# Market: Construction Subcontractor Operations Software (US)
slug: construction-subcontractor-ops-software-us

## 2026-06-26 revision — idea: The construction subcontractor's "get paid on time" hub (score: 97)
*   **TAM/SAM/SOM:** TAM: >$2T US construction market. SAM: ~2.3M specialty trade firms x $1,788/yr = ~$4.1B. SOM (2-yr): 0.1% of SAM = ~$4.1M ARR.
*   **Growth:** Steady at 4-5% CAGR, tied to broader construction market growth.
*   **Fragmentation:** Buyer base is highly fragmented. The top 50 specialty contractors account for <10% of revenue, leaving millions of SMBs.
*   **Margin pool:** Margin sits with the party that can manage cash flow effectively. Subcontractors lose margin to debt, factoring, and administrative overhead caused by payment friction.
*   **Distribution control:** GCs control project flow and often mandate which software is used for invoicing (e.g., Procore, Textura). However, the subcontractor controls their own internal operational tools.
*   **Incumbents and inertia:** Incumbents (Procore, Oracle) are built for and sell to the GC. Their business model is not aligned with solving the subcontractor's core pain. This creates a structural gap for a sub-first tool.
*   **Regulatory / platform risk:** Low platform risk. High regulatory complexity due to state-by-state variations in lien laws, which is both a barrier to entry and a source of moat if solved correctly.
*   **Key sources:** https://www.levelset.com/blog/construction-payment-statistics/, https://www.census.gov/library/stories/2021/05/what-is-a-specialty-trade-contractor.html, https://www.procore.com/press/procore-acquires-levelset
