# Market: US Small Job Shop Quoting Software
slug: us-small-job-shop-quoting-software

## 2026-08-11 revision — idea: The small job shop's "no-regret" quote generator (score: 98)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM: $113B+ US Metal Fabrication Market / SAM: 20,000 small shops × $2,988/year = $59.76M / SOM (Yr 3): 200 shops x $2,988/year = $597k ARR.
- Growth: Market growing at ~5.2% CAGR, driven by infrastructure spending and reshoring.
- Fragmentation: Highly fragmented. 17,000-29,000+ shops in the US, with over 50% having fewer than 5 employees. No dominant players in the small shop segment.
- Margin pool: Margin sits with the shop owner, but is constantly at risk due to quoting errors and material price volatility. Well-run shops have 15-35% net margins.
- Distribution control: No single entity controls distribution. Access is via content marketing, community engagement in online forums, and direct outreach.
- Incumbents and inertia: Incumbents (ERPs like ProShop, high-end tools like Paperless Parts) are too complex and expensive for the long tail. Inertia (spreadsheets) is the main competitor, but is increasingly risky.
- Regulatory / platform risk: Low. This is a pure B2B workflow tool not dependent on any single platform or regulation.
