# Market: US DME Supplier Medicare Compliance Software
slug: us-dme-supplier-medicare-compliance-software

## 2026-08-09 revision — idea: The DME supplier's "get paid by Medicare" evidence locker (score: 98)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM: $74.6B (US DME Market 2026). SAM: ~4,000 independent suppliers × $3,600/year = ~$14.4M ARR. SOM: 100 suppliers x $3,600/year = $360k ARR.
- Growth: Market growing at ~6% CAGR, but number of independent suppliers is consolidating under regulatory and reimbursement pressure.
- Fragmentation: Highly fragmented base of thousands of small, local suppliers.
- Margin pool: Sits with suppliers who can effectively manage documentation to minimize claim denials and accelerate cash flow. High improper payment rate (24.1%) indicates significant value is being lost.
- Distribution control: Incumbents (Brightree, WellSky) have deep penetration but are vulnerable to more focused, user-friendly tools. Trust is also held by billing consultants and industry associations.
- Incumbents and inertia: Incumbents are large, complex, and expensive. Many small suppliers rely on manual processes. The inertia of "the way we've always done it" is high, but the increasing financial pressure creates a strong incentive to change.
- Regulatory / platform risk: High. The business exists because of complex Medicare rules. A simplification of these rules would reduce the need for the product. However, the trend is toward *more* scrutiny, not less.
