# Market: DME Supplier Operations & Compliance Software (US)
slug: dme-supplier-compliance-software-us

## 2026-06-24 revision — idea: The Durable Medical Equipment (DME) Supplier's "Audit-Proof" Delivery Log (score: 97)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM: $70.7B (equipment market). SAM: $14.3M (8,005 suppliers x $1,788/yr). SOM: $429k (3% of SAM). Sources: Fortune Business Insights, Federal Register.
- Growth: Equipment market growing at 5.5% CAGR. Number of traditional suppliers has decreased, indicating consolidation and increasing professionalism among survivors. Source: Fortune Business Insights, Federal Register.
- Fragmentation: Highly fragmented long-tail of ~8,000 small, independent suppliers.
- Margin pool: Suppliers operate on thin 8-15% margins and are highly motivated to prevent revenue loss from audits. Software margins are 85%+.
- Distribution control: Decentralized. Access via industry publications (HME News), trade associations, and partnerships with billing/compliance consultants.
- Incumbents and inertia: Incumbents (Brightree) sell complex, expensive ERPs. Inertia is reliance on manual, paper-based systems. The gap is for a simple, cheap, mobile-first compliance point solution.
- Regulatory / platform risk: High. The entire business is predicated on Medicare (CMS) documentation rules. A change in those rules could eliminate the need for the product.
