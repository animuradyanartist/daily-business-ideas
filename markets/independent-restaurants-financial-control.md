# Market: Independent Restaurants (Financial Control)
slug: independent-restaurants-financial-control

## 2026-06-01 revision — idea: The Restaurant Owner's Prime Cost Control Kit (score: 78)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM: ~749k US restaurants. SAM: ~412k US independent restaurants ($41M market @ $100/unit). SOM: 0.25% of SAM = ~1,031 customers or ~$150k revenue.
- Growth: Contracting at -2.3% to -2.5% annually for independents, which intensifies the pain for survivors.
- Fragmentation: Highly fragmented buyer side (7 in 10 are single-unit operations). Fragmented supplier side for non-food items.
- Margin pool: The buyer's net profit margin is extremely thin (3-5%). The value is captured by helping them control prime costs (55-65% of revenue).
- Distribution control: No single entity controls distribution. Access is through fragmented online communities, industry media, and food service distributors.
- Incumbents and inertia: Incumbent SaaS (Toast, R365) are too complex and expensive. Incumbent services (consultants) are prohibitively expensive. This leaves a gap for a simple, low-cost toolkit.
- Regulatory / platform risk: Low. This is an educational/process product, not financial advice. No platform dependency.
