# Market: US Freight Broker Compliance Software
slug: us-freight-broker-compliance-software

## 2026-09-29 revision — idea: The Freight Broker's "Fraud-Proof" Carrier Onboarding Hub (score: 100)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM: ~$21B (total brokerage market). SAM: ~$27M (15k small brokers x $1,800/yr). SOM: ~$135k (Year 1 goal).
- Growth: Market growing at 7-8.6% CAGR. Digital brokerage segment growing at 16.75% CAGR.
- Fragmentation: Highly fragmented. Over 26,000 brokers, the vast majority are small businesses.
- Margin pool: Margin sits with software providers who can reduce catastrophic financial risk (fraud) and increase operational speed.
- Distribution control: Uncontrolled. Brokers can be reached directly via online communities and industry associations.
- Incumbents and inertia: Incumbents are priced for the enterprise (MyCarrierPackets, Highway, RMIS), leaving a large affordability and simplicity gap for the SMB segment.
- Regulatory / platform risk: Risk is a positive driver. Increased FMCSA regulation and expanded legal liability (SCOTUS ruling) are creating strong demand for compliance and vetting tools.
