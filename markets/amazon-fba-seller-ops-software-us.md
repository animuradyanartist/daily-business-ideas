# Market: Amazon FBA Seller Operations Software (US)
slug: amazon-fba-seller-ops-software-us

## 2026-06-28 revision — idea: The FBA Seller's "Inventory Reconciliation & Reimbursement" Hub (score: 95)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM: ~$1.96B (1.65M US sellers x $1,188/yr). SAM: ~$705.7M (594k sellers >$100k/yr x $1,188/yr). SOM (Y3): ~$2.1M.
- Growth: Market is maturing. New seller growth is slow, but the number of professional sellers (>$1M/yr) is growing quickly (+36% in 2025).
- Fragmentation: Highly fragmented customer base of hundreds of thousands of small and medium-sized businesses.
- Margin pool: Sellers pay 40-50%+ of revenue to Amazon in fees. They will pay software providers to optimize the remaining margin by recovering lost revenue or improving efficiency.
- Distribution control: Amazon controls the platform and API access. Customer access is via content marketing and advertising in seller-focused communities.
- Incumbents and inertia: Incumbents are primarily high-cost service models (GETIDA) or bloated all-in-one SaaS suites (Helium 10). The gap is for a focused, best-in-class, flat-rate SaaS tool.
- Regulatory / platform risk: Extremely high platform risk. The business is entirely dependent on Amazon's API policies and business practices.
