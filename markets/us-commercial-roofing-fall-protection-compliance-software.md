## 2026-10-27 revision — idea: The Commercial Roofing Contractor's "Fall Protection Audit-Ready" Daily Log (score: 96)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM: $26.66B (US commercial roofing market 2026). SAM: 7,800 independent commercial roofing contractors. SOM: $32.76M annually (7,800 contractors * $350/month * 12 months).
- Growth: 4.92% CAGR from 2026-2035 (projected for commercial roofing market). Direction: growing.
- Fragmentation: Highly fragmented, top 100 account for ~18% market share.
- Margin pool: Preventing catastrophic OSHA fines (up to $478,000 per incident) and reducing exorbitant workers' compensation/liability insurance premiums ($48,000-$90,000 annually for a 10-person crew).
- Distribution control: Industry associations (NRCA), general construction software providers (Procore), and safety consultants.
- Incumbents and inertia: Generalist construction safety software (Procore Safety, SafetyCulture) are too broad/expensive for SMBs. Manual paper logs are common but indefensible. Inertia is due to existing (suboptimal) processes and resistance to new tech.
- Regulatory / platform risk: High OSHA enforcement (especially "instance-by-instance" citations) is a demand driver, not a risk. No platform dependency.
