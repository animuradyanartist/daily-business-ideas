# Market: CPG Deduction Management Software (US)
slug: cpg-deduction-management-software

## 2026-06-19 revision — idea: The CPG Brand's "Chargeback Defender" Kit (score: 97)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM: >$1.5T US CPG Market, creating billions in addressable pain (invalid deductions). SAM: ~20,000 emerging brands x $3k/yr = $60M/yr. SOM (Yr 2): 100 brands x $3k/yr = $300k ARR.
- Growth: Steady CPG market growth (~3.5% CAGR) combined with increasing automation of deductions by retailers expands the problem space.
- Fragmentation: Highly fragmented buyer base of thousands of small CPG brands. Concentrated power with major retailers/distributors (Walmart, Kroger, UNFI, KeHE) who create the problem.
- Margin pool: The margin sits in the ~40-60% of deductions that are invalid and recoverable. Software takes a small subscription fee to help brands reclaim this lost profit.
- Distribution control: Customer access is via direct marketing to CPG communities (e.g., Startup CPG), content marketing/SEO, and partnerships with fractional CFOs/accountants.
- Incumbents and inertia: Incumbents (Vividly, CPGvision) sell large, complex, expensive Trade Promotion Management suites to enterprise CPGs, leaving the emerging brand segment underserved by simpler, more affordable tools.
- Regulatory / platform risk: High platform risk. The business model relies on accessing data from retailer/distributor portals which can change or be restricted at any time.
