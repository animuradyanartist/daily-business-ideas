# Market: Small DoD Contractor CMMC Compliance Software (US)
slug: small-dod-contractor-cmmc-compliance-software-us

## 2026-07-01 revision — idea: The small government contractor's "CMMC audit-ready" evidence locker (score: 98)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM: >$700B federal contracting market. SAM: ~75k small business government vendors x ~$6k/yr = ~$450M. SOM: Capture 0.5% = $2.25M ARR.
- Growth: High growth driven by a mandatory, multi-year regulatory rollout (CMMC 2.0) that began in late 2025.
- Fragmentation: Buyer base is highly fragmented (tens of thousands of small businesses). Supplier side for software is emerging, with a few startups and legacy GRC players.
- Margin pool: Sits with software and expert consultants. Contractors pay to de-risk the audit and reduce the massive internal labor cost of compliance.
- Distribution control: No single player controls distribution. Reachable via content marketing (SEO), targeted ads (LinkedIn), and partnerships with the fragmented consultant ecosystem (RPOs).
- Incumbents and inertia: Incumbents are expensive consultants (unscalable) and complex enterprise GRC platforms (overkill for SMBs). Inertia is using SharePoint/shared drives, which is a poor, anxiety-inducing solution for a high-stakes audit.
- Regulatory / platform risk: High. The entire market is a creation of DoD regulation. Any significant change or delay to the CMMC program directly impacts the opportunity.
