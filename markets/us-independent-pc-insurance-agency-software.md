# Market: US Independent P&C Insurance Agency Software
slug: us-independent-pc-insurance-agency-software

## 2026-09-27 revision — idea: The Commercial Insurance Broker's "E&O-Proof" Statement of Values (SOV) Hub (score: 96)
*   TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM: $1.96B (Global insurance broker software). SAM: $55.5M (37k US agencies x $1.5k ACV). SOM (Y2): $300k.
*   Growth: Market is mature but software spend is increasing with insurtech adoption. Agency count is consolidating slightly but profitable.
*   Fragmentation: Highly fragmented buyer base. ~37,000 independent P&C agencies, with ~30,000 of them being smaller businesses.
*   Margin pool: Margin sits with the brokers (commissions) and the carriers (premiums). Brokers have budget for software that protects commissions and reduces risk.
*   Distribution control: Brokers own the end-customer relationship. Distribution requires selling directly to the thousands of independent agencies.
*   Incumbents and inertia: Dominated by large, legacy Agency Management Systems (Vertafore, Applied Systems). They are slow-moving systems of record, leaving gaps for modern, best-in-class workflow tools to unbundle specific, high-pain processes like data collection.
*   Regulatory / platform risk: Low. This is a workflow tool that sits on top of the regulated industry, not within it. The primary risk is dependence on future integration with AMS platforms to move upmarket.
