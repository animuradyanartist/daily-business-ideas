# Market: Small Law Firm Compliance Software (US)
slug: small-law-firm-compliance-software

## 2026-06-18 revision — idea: The Small Law Firm's "IOLTA Compliance" Hub (score: 96)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM: $2.1B (US LPMS Market). SAM: 300,000 small firms x $588/yr = $176.4M. SOM (Yr 3): 3,000 firms x $588/yr = $1.76M.
- Growth: Market growing at ~10.9% CAGR.
- Fragmentation: Buyer market is highly fragmented; ~76% of private practice lawyers are in firms of 10 or fewer.
- Margin pool: High gross margins (~85-90%) sit with the software provider. Value is in mitigating catastrophic compliance risk.
- Distribution control: Decentralized. Access through content marketing, legal communities (r/LawFirm), and partnerships with state bar associations.
- Incumbents and inertia: Incumbents (Clio, MyCase) are large, all-in-one suites. They are often complex and their trust accounting modules can be clunky. Inertia is high, but the pain of compliance failure creates opportunities for a focused, best-in-class tool.
- Regulatory / platform risk: Low. The business is built on top of state bar association rules; a simplification of these rules is the primary risk, but is highly unlikely.
