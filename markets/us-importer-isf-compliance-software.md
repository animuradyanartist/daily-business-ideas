# Market: US Importer ISF Compliance Software
slug: us-importer-isf-compliance-software

## 2026-09-05 revision — idea: The US Importer's "ISF 10+2" Filing Preparation Hub (score: 100)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM: 28M+ annual container imports. SAM: 100,000 SMB importers × $588/year = $58.8M. SOM: 500 importers x $588/year = $294k ARR.
- Growth: Tied to US import volumes, which are growing long-term. More importantly, growth is driven by increasing CBP enforcement of existing rules.
- Fragmentation: Buyer base of importers is highly fragmented, from solo FBA sellers to mid-sized companies.
- Margin pool: Sits with customs brokers who charge a per-filing service fee and software providers who sell to brokers.
- Distribution control: Customs brokers currently own the customer relationship for filing. Direct-to-importer distribution is possible via paid search and content marketing.
- Incumbents and inertia: Incumbents (Descartes, etc.) build complex, expensive software for licensed brokers, not simple, affordable tools for the importers themselves. Brokers are service providers and unlikely to build their own SaaS.
- Regulatory / platform risk: Low. The ISF 10+2 rule is a mature, stable regulation in place since 2009.
