# Market: US Clinical Research Site Compliance Software
slug: us-clinical-research-site-compliance-software

## 2026-09-30 revision — idea: The Independent Clinical Research Site's "FDA-Proof" Investigator Site File Hub (score: 100)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM: 20,000 global sites × $3,600 ARPA = $72M. SAM: 1,414 US independent sites × $3,600 ARPA = $5.1M. SOM (Yr 3): 141 sites × $3,600 ARPA = $507k ARR.
- Growth: Global clinical trials market estimated at $131.8B in 2026, growing to $200B+ by 2036. The underlying driver is increasing trial volume and complexity. eISF market itself projected at 14.5% CAGR.
- Fragmentation: Highly fragmented. While large networks exist, there are thousands of independent sites. A database identified 1,414 distinct site companies in the US alone.
- Margin pool: Sits with large enterprise software vendors (Veeva, Florence) selling expensive, complex systems to pharmaceutical sponsors.
- Distribution control: Incumbents control distribution via their existing relationships with sponsors. Opportunity is to go direct-to-site via industry associations (SCRS, ACRP).
- Incumbents and inertia: Incumbents are sponsor-focused. Their site-facing products are complex or, in Veeva's case, a free data funnel for their paying sponsor customers. This leaves a gap for a simple, affordable, site-centric tool.
- Regulatory / platform risk: Low. FDA's 21 CFR Part 11 clearly defines the requirements. The risk is not regulatory but competitive, primarily from Veeva's free offering.
