# Market: US Independent Funeral Home Compliance Software
slug: us-independent-funeral-home-compliance-software

## 2026-09-26 revision — idea: The Independent Funeral Home's "Lawsuit-Proof" Cremation Chain of Custody Log (score: 100)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM: $22.8B US funeral homes market. SAM: ~16,375 independent firms x ~$3k/yr = ~$49M ARR. SOM (Yr 3): ~325 firms = ~$971k ARR. (Sources: IBISWorld, NFDA)
- Growth: Market is stable, but the underlying driver (cremation rate) is growing rapidly from ~60% to ~80% of cases by 2045, increasing the risk and need for tooling. (Source: CANA)
- Fragmentation: Highly fragmented. 89.1% of 18,378 US funeral homes are privately owned. (Source: NFDA)
- Margin pool: Value is in de-risking the business from catastrophic, multi-million dollar lawsuits and reputational ruin. The software cost is negligible compared to the potential liability.
- Distribution control: Access is controlled by industry associations (NFDA, CANA) and trust. No single channel dominates.
- Incumbents and inertia: Incumbents (Passare, etc.) sell large, complex suites. Inertia is strong due to the conservative, slow-adopting nature of the industry and reliance on paper-based systems that are "good enough." This leaves a gap for a simple, best-in-class tool that solves the highest-risk problem.
- Regulatory / platform risk: Low. Risk is primarily on the business side (slow adoption). Regulation is a tailwind, with states like Colorado mandating more rigorous digital-ready tracking.
- Key sources: https://www.nfda.org/news/statistics, https://www.cremationassociation.org/blog/post/2024/05/29/new-projections-show-cremation-rate-on-the-rise, https://dpo.colorado.gov/sites/dpo/files/Mortuary%20Science%20Rule%20400%20eff%2001-01-2024.pdf
