# Market: US Agricultural H-2A Visa Compliance Software
slug: us-agricultural-h2a-compliance-software

## 2026-09-15 revision — idea: The Agricultural Employer's "Audit-Ready" H-2A Visa Compliance Hub (score: 100)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM: ~19,000 H-2A employers. SAM: ~15,200 employers without in-house legal/HR teams, representing a ~$53M market at a $3,500 ARPA. SOM (Yr 3): Capture 2% for ~$1.05M ARR.
- Growth: The H-2A program's use has quadrupled in the last decade and is seen as essential to US agriculture, indicating sustained growth.
- Fragmentation: Buyer base is highly fragmented, consisting of thousands of farms nationwide, making it suitable for a product-led GTM.
- Margin pool: Margin currently sits with high-cost, high-touch service providers (lawyers, agents). Software can automate the most scalable part of the work (record-keeping) and capture this margin.
- Distribution control: No single entity controls distribution. Access is through industry associations, publications, and direct online marketing.
- Incumbents and inertia: Incumbents are service businesses, not software companies. Their model is based on manual work and billable hours, making them vulnerable to a scalable, automated SaaS product.
- Regulatory / platform risk: The core risk is a fundamental simplification of the H-2A program, which is unlikely given current legislative and administrative trends. The complexity of the regulation is the opportunity.
