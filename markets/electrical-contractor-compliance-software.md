# Market: Electrical Contractor Compliance Software (US)
slug: electrical-contractor-compliance-software

## 2026-06-17 revision — idea: The Electrical Contractor's Apprentice Compliance Log (score: 93)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM: ~$44.2M (122k apprentices x $360/yr). SAM: ~$38.9M (targeting 88% of firms <20 employees). SOM (Yr 3): ~$778k. Method: # of firms x avg. apprentices/firm x ARPA. Sources:.
- Growth: Market growing at ~2.4% annually, driven by construction and electrification trends. Sources:.
- Fragmentation: Highly fragmented. ~81,000+ firms, the vast majority with <20 employees. Ideal for bottom-up, self-serve SaaS. Sources:.
- Margin pool: Contractors operate on ~7.5% net margins and are sensitive to non-billable admin overhead and risks to their labor pipeline. The value is in de-risking a core asset (apprentice licensure). Sources:.
- Distribution control: No single channel controls access. Reachable via SEO, content marketing, and targeted ads in online trade communities.
- Incumbents and inertia: Incumbents are either too generic (payroll time trackers like QuickBooks Time) or too enterprise-focused (union/JATC systems like TradeSchool). They do not serve the small contractor's specific state compliance workflow well. Sources:.
- Regulatory / platform risk: Low. The system is built on top of state regulations, which are slow to change. The risk is that states could offer their own free tracking tools, but this is unlikely given government software procurement cycles.
