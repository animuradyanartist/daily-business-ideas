# Market: US Independent Auto Dealer OFAC Compliance Software
slug: us-independent-auto-dealer-ofac-compliance-software

## 2026-09-25 revision — idea: The Independent Auto Dealer's "10-Year Audit-Proof" OFAC Compliance Hub (score: 85)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM: $49.7M (69k dealers x $720). SAM: $37.4M (52k independent dealers x $720). SOM (Yr 3): $1.5M. Method: Bottom-up based on dealer counts and estimated ARPA. Sources: flai.com, blueandco.com.
- Growth: Market size is stable, but the regulatory burden is increasing. The 2024 extension of the OFAC record retention requirement to 10 years is a key growth driver for this niche. Source: movemetal.com, troutman.com.
- Fragmentation: The independent dealer segment is highly fragmented, with ~52,000 active operators, most of whom are small businesses.
- Margin pool: Margin currently sits with bundled DMS and credit report providers who offer OFAC checks as a feature. The opportunity is to unbundle the high-stakes archiving component.
- Distribution control: Distribution is influenced by industry associations (NIADA, state IADAs) and a network of consultants and F&I product providers.
- Incumbents and inertia: Incumbents are large DMS platforms (CDK, Dealertrack, DealerCenter) and credit bureaus (700Credit). They are slow to react to specific regulatory changes and are not focused on a best-in-class user experience for a single compliance feature like long-term archiving.
- Regulatory / platform risk: Low. The business exists because of the regulation. The primary risk is a future change that removes or simplifies the requirement.
