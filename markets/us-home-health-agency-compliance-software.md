# Market: US Home Health Agency Compliance Software
slug: us-home-health-agency-compliance-software

## 2026-08-15 revision — idea: The Home Health Agency's "Get Paid by Medicaid" Evidence Locker (score: 98)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM: $191.6B US Home Care market. SAM: ~8,260 small/mid-size agencies x $3,588/yr = $29.6M/yr. SOM: 41 agencies x $3,588/yr = $147k/yr.
- Growth: 6.7% CAGR, driven by aging US population.
- Fragmentation: Highly fragmented market of ~492k providers, with a long tail of small, independent agencies.
- Margin pool: Sits with software providers who can reduce revenue loss and administrative waste for agencies facing reimbursement pressure.
- Distribution control: Incumbent EMRs (Axxess, WellSky) have strong control but leave a gap for specialized tools marketed directly to owners/billers via content and digital ads.
- Incumbents and inertia: Incumbents offer EVV as a feature in a complex EMR, but fail to solve the "pre-submission audit" problem, as evidenced by high denial rates. Inertia is high, but the acute pain of cash-flow disruption and clawback risk can overcome it.
- Regulatory / platform risk: High dependence on state-by-state Medicaid rules and EVV systems requires constant monitoring and updates. A federal policy change could alter the landscape.
