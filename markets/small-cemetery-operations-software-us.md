# Market: Small Cemetery Operations Software (US)
slug: small-cemetery-operations-software-us

## 2026-06-30 revision — idea: The Small Cemetery's "Plot & Records" Management Hub (score: 94)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM: $6.3B US Cemetery Services market. SAM: $11.8M - $171M/year (10k-144k small cemeteries x ~$1.2k/yr). SOM (Yr 2): ~$178k.
- Growth: Mature market (1.9% CAGR), but strong internal driver of digitization to replace aging paper records.
- Fragmentation: Highly fragmented buyer base of thousands of small, independent cemeteries run by non-profits, churches, and municipalities.
- Margin pool: Sits with the software provider due to the high liability cost of the alternative (manual errors). High gross margins (>90%) are standard.
- Distribution control: No single entity controls distribution. Access is through direct outreach, content marketing, and partnerships with state-level associations.
- Incumbents and inertia: Incumbents (PlotBox, CIMS) are too expensive and complex for the long tail. The primary competitor is inertia and the "good enough" nature of existing spreadsheets, which is overcome by focusing on liability and risk.
- Regulatory / platform risk: Very low. This is a B2B tool for internal record-keeping with no platform dependency or major regulatory overhead.
