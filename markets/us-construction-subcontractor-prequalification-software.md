# Market: US Construction Subcontractor Prequalification Software
slug: us-construction-subcontractor-prequalification-software

## 2026-08-29 revision — idea: The Construction Subcontractor's "Bid-Ready" Prequalification Packet Generator (score: 99)
*   **TAM/SAM/SOM:** TAM: >$7.2B (Global Construction Mgmt Software). SAM: ~$594M (500k US specialty subs x $1,188/yr). SOM (Yr 2): ~$594k (500 subs).
*   **Growth:** The underlying construction management software market is growing at ~9-12% CAGR, driven by digitization.
*   **Fragmentation:** Buyer base is extremely fragmented (hundreds of thousands of small businesses). Suppliers (GCs) are more concentrated.
*   **Margin pool:** Margin sits with the party that can reduce the most expensive friction. Here, it's saving expensive administrative labor and unlocking access to six- and seven-figure contracts.
*   **Distribution control:** GCs control access to projects and can mandate the use of their preferred portals (e.g., TradeTapp). The opportunity is to create a tool that sits on the subcontractor's side and feeds into all portals.
*   **Incumbents and inertia:** Incumbents like Autodesk are focused on selling enterprise platforms to GCs. They have little incentive to build a low-cost tool that solves a pain point for the subcontractor, who is not their primary paying customer.
*   **Regulatory / platform risk:** Low. Prequalification is a business process, not a government regulation. There is some platform risk if major GC portals block data-filling tools, but this is unlikely.
