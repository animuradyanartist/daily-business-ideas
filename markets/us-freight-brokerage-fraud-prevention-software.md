# Market: US Freight Brokerage Fraud Prevention Software
slug: us-freight-brokerage-fraud-prevention-software

## 2026-08-20 revision — idea: The Freight Broker's "Anti-Double-Brokering" Carrier Verification System (score: 99)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM: ~$800M+ (annual fraud losses). SAM: ~$107M (30k SMBs x $3.6k/yr). SOM: ~$538k (150 SMBs).
- Growth: High. Driven by increasing sophistication of fraud and rising value of cargo, making prevention more critical.
- Fragmentation: Buyer side is highly fragmented with over 77,000 brokerages, creating an opportunity for a self-serve SaaS model.
- Margin pool: Sits with the brokerages who stand to lose hundreds of thousands of dollars from a single fraudulent event. They pay to protect their revenue and existence.
- Distribution control: Controlled by large load boards (DAT, Truckstop) and TMS providers. A go-to-market strategy must include direct outreach and content marketing, with a long-term goal of integrating with these platforms.
- Incumbents and inertia: Incumbent TMS providers have basic, static verification. They are not focused on the rapidly evolving, specialized arms race of fraud detection, leaving a gap for a dedicated, best-in-class solution.
- Regulatory / platform risk: Low regulatory risk. High platform risk if dependent on APIs from load boards, but the model can work via direct outreach and a standalone web app/extension. Recent legal rulings *increase* demand.
