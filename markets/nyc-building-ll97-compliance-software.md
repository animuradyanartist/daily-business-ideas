# Market: NYC Building Local Law 97 Compliance Software
slug: nyc-building-ll97-compliance-software

## 2026-09-04 revision — idea: The NYC Building Owner's "Local Law 97" Compliance Forecaster (score: 99)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM: Global ESG Software Market ($4.78B in 2026). SAM: ~50,000 NYC buildings × ~$2,500/yr = $125M annually. SOM (Y1): 100 buildings x $2,500/yr = $250k ARR.
- Growth: Geographically fixed, but demand intensity is rapidly increasing due to the approaching 2030 deadline when emissions limits tighten and an estimated 57%+ of buildings will become non-compliant.
- Fragmentation: Buyer side is fragmented (individual owners, thousands of co-op boards) but also has points of concentration (large property management firms).
- Margin pool: Sits with software providers who can unbundle high-cost consulting services and with the consultants themselves who provide bespoke analysis and professional sign-off.
- Distribution control: Distribution is influenced by the ecosystem of registered design professionals (architects/engineers) who must certify reports, and large property management firms who manage portfolios of buildings.
- Incumbents and inertia: Incumbents are high-touch consulting firms and broad ESG platforms. They leave open a gap for a focused, self-serve financial planning and scenario-modeling tool that empowers owners *before* they engage expensive service providers.
- Regulatory / platform risk: Low. The primary risk is a repeal or weakening of Local Law 97, which is unlikely given it is a cornerstone of NYC's climate policy.
