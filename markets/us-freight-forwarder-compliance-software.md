# Market: US Freight Forwarder & Customs Broker Compliance Software
slug: us-freight-forwarder-compliance-software

## 2026-09-02 revision — idea: The "Audit-Ready" Freight Forwarder's Document Hub (score: 100)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM: $42.5B (US Freight Forwarding Market). SAM: $78.7M (21,873 employer firms x $3.6k ACV). SOM (Y2): $785k.
- Growth: US market growing at 6.27% CAGR.
- Fragmentation: Highly fragmented market of ~77k total businesses, with a core of ~22k employer establishments. Dominated by one software incumbent.
- Margin pool: Margin sits with the forwarders/brokers who successfully manage complex compliance and logistics. It is destroyed by fines, delays, and manual error.
- Distribution control: Distribution is controlled by the incumbent TMS, CargoWise. The opportunity is to reach customers directly through channels discussing their dissatisfaction with the incumbent.
- Incumbents and inertia: CargoWise is the dominant incumbent. Their mandatory and unpopular pricing change on Dec 1, 2025, has broken inertia and created an active search for alternatives.
- Regulatory / platform risk: Low. The business helps customers comply with existing, stable government regulations (CBP).
