# Market: Independent Catastrophe Adjuster Software (US)
slug: independent-cat-adjuster-software-us

## 2026-06-29 revision — idea: The Independent Catastrophe Insurance Adjuster's "Claim File" Builder (score: 96)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM: ~$5.4B US Insurtech market. SAM: 10,000 independent CAT adjusters × $1,788/year = $17.8M. SOM: 300 adjusters × $1,788/year = ~$536k ARR.
- Growth: Stable, event-driven (tied to natural disasters). The underlying pressure from workload and burnout is increasing.
- Fragmentation: Highly fragmented base of thousands of solo 1099 contractors who make their own purchasing decisions.
- Margin pool: The buyer's income is directly tied to their personal throughput. They will pay for tools that give them leverage and speed, as the ROI is immediate and significant.
- Distribution control: Decentralized. Access is through targeted advertising and content marketing in niche online communities and via industry training influencers.
- Incumbents and inertia: Incumbents focus on estimation (Xactimate) or are general-purpose (CompanyCam). The workflow of assembling the complete claim file (photos + narrative) is underserved by a purpose-built tool.
- Regulatory / platform risk: Low. The core deliverable (a PDF report) is platform-agnostic. Work is driven by insurance carriers, but the independent adjuster chooses their own efficiency tools.
