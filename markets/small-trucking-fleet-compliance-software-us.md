# Market: Small Trucking Fleet Compliance Software (US)
slug: small-trucking-fleet-compliance-software-us

## 2026-06-27 revision — idea: The Small Trucking Fleet's "DOT-Audit-Ready" Compliance Hub (score: 97)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM: $906B US trucking industry revenue. SAM: ~727,500 carriers (1-20 trucks) × $2,388/year = $1.73B. SOM: 0.1% of SAM = $1.73M ARR.
- Growth: Mature, stable industry. Growth in compliance need is driven by stricter, more data-driven enforcement and rising fines, not fleet expansion.
- Fragmentation: Extreme buyer fragmentation. 91.5% of US motor carriers operate 10 or fewer trucks.
- Margin pool: Margin sits with software and service providers who can demonstrably reduce the risk of catastrophic fines ($7k+ avg) and business shutdowns for a monthly fee that is a fraction of the potential cost.
- Distribution control: No single player controls distribution. The market is reached through fragmented online communities, content marketing (YouTube), and industry-specific media.
- Incumbents and inertia: Incumbents are either too complex and expensive for small fleets (J.J. Keller) or are focused on a different part of the problem—in-cab/driver compliance vs. back-office/entity compliance (Motive, Samsara).
- Regulatory / platform risk: Low. The business exists to service federal regulations, so stricter rules are a tailwind. It is a standalone SaaS with no platform dependency.
