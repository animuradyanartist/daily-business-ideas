# Market: US Boutique Architecture Firm Construction Administration Software
slug: us-boutique-architecture-firm-construction-admin-software

## 2026-07-24 revision — idea: The boutique architecture firm's "submittal & RFI" tracker (score: 96)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM: ~$65.2B (US architecture services market). SAM: ~$34M (14,250 small firms x $2,388/yr). SOM (Y2): ~$513k (215 firms).
- Growth: Market growing at 1.5-4.3% CAGR, supported by public infrastructure spending.
- Fragmentation: Highly fragmented buyer market. 75% of the ~19,000 US architecture firms have fewer than 10 employees.
- Margin pool: Margin sits with the software provider who can solve a high-risk workflow for a flat, predictable fee, saving the firm from the high variable cost of errors ($1,080+ per RFI) or expensive enterprise software ($15k+).
- Distribution control: No single party controls distribution. Reach is through targeted digital marketing, content, and industry associations (AIA).
- Incumbents and inertia: Incumbents (Procore, Autodesk) are focused on enterprise and priced based on construction volume, making them inaccessible to small firms. The primary inertia is the use of "free" but high-risk spreadsheets and email.
- Regulatory / platform risk: None.
