slug: us-commercial-property-fire-safety-compliance-software

## 2026-10-20 revision — idea: The Commercial Property Manager's "Audit-Ready" Fire Safety Log Hub (score: 85)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: U.S. property management services market $122.02B (2025), growing to $184.25B (2033). U.S. property management software market $7.51B (2025). SAM for dedicated fire safety software for small/mid-sized commercial property managers estimated at $250M annually (41,590 managers * 10 properties/manager * $50/property/month * 12 months).
- Growth: Projected CAGR of 5.4% from 2026 to 2033 for property management services, driven by digital transformation.
- Fragmentation: Highly fragmented at the small to mid-sized commercial property management firm level, with many independent operators and smaller portfolios.
- Margin pool: Derived from mitigating substantial financial penalties (OSHA fines up to $165k), avoiding operational shutdowns, reducing increased insurance premiums, and ensuring tenant safety.
- Distribution control: Industry associations (BOMA International, CAI), direct online search, and specialized software vendor channels.
- Incumbents and inertia: Generic CMMS/property management software lacks specific depth; specialized inspection tools are often point solutions; enterprise systems are too complex/expensive for target SMBs. Inertia from manual processes and spreadsheets.
- Regulatory / platform risk: Stable regulatory environment defined by NFPA 10, 25, 72, and OSHA (29 CFR 1910.157), alongside local fire codes, with predictable compliance landscape.
