# Market: US DoD Contractor CMMC Compliance Software
slug: us-dod-contractor-cmmc-compliance-software

## 2026-08-17 revision — idea: The Government Contractor's "CMMC Level 1" Compliance Evidence Locker (score: 98)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM: ~205k US gov contractors. SAM: ~139k companies requiring CMMC Level 1. SOM (Yr 3): ~700 companies.
- Growth: Market created by CMMC 2.0 regulation (phased rollout started late 2025). Sustained by annual self-attestation requirement.
- Fragmentation: Highly fragmented buyer base of tens of thousands of SMBs.
- Margin pool: Sits with the contractor's ability to remain eligible for DoD contracts. The cost of failure is total revenue loss and potential FCA fines.
- Distribution control: No single entity controls access. Reachable via content marketing, targeted digital ads, and partnerships with prime contractors.
- Incumbents and inertia: Incumbents are high-cost consultants and complex enterprise GRC software. They leave a gap for a simple, affordable, self-serve tool for the lowest compliance tier (Level 1).
- Regulatory / platform risk: High. The entire market is a creation of DoD regulation (CMMC), which can be changed, delayed, or repealed.
