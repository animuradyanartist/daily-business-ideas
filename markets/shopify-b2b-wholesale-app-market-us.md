# Market: Shopify B2B/Wholesale App Market (US)
slug: shopify-b2b-wholesale-app-market-us

## 2026-07-11 revision — idea: The high-growth Shopify brand's "wholesale order" portal (score: 98)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM: ~$189.6M (200k merchants × $948/yr). SAM: ~$94.8M (100k merchants × $948/yr). SOM (Yr 3): ~$948k. Method based on % of total Shopify merchants pursuing B2B and average app price.
- Growth: Strong tailwinds. Growth of e-commerce and DTC brands diversifying into wholesale. Shopify's expansion of B2B features to all plans in 2026 is a market accelerant.
- Fragmentation: Buyer base is highly fragmented (hundreds of thousands of individual merchants).
- Margin pool: High. Software gross margins are >90%. Buyers are businesses paying for a revenue-enabling tool, creating strong pricing power.
- Distribution control: Shopify controls distribution through its App Store. Success is dependent on mastering this channel via SEO, ads, and reviews.
- Incumbents and inertia: Incumbents exist but often have dated UX. Shopify's native solution is basic. A gap exists for a modern, mid-market solution focused on user experience.
- Regulatory / platform risk: High platform risk. Shopify could change its API or improve native features to compete directly.
- Key sources: https://apps.shopify.com/, https://www.shopify.com/b2b, https://community.shopify.com/
