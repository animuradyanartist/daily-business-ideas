# Market: Local Service Business Call Tracking Software
slug: local-service-business-call-tracking

## 2026-06-06 revision — idea: The Local Service Business "Call Source" Tracking Kit (score: 85)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM: $10.84B (global call tracking software market). SAM: $468M (1M US service businesses x $39/mo). SOM: $936k (2k customers x $39/mo). Sources:
- Growth: ~10% CAGR, driven by increased digital ad spend by SMBs. Source:
- Fragmentation: The buyer base is extremely fragmented, consisting of millions of independent small businesses.
- Margin pool: Margin sits with the software provider, who buys wholesale telephony services (from Twilio, etc.) and sells a complete solution. Gross margins are >80%.
- Distribution control: Google controls the primary customer acquisition channels (Search, Ads, GBP).
- Incumbents and inertia: Incumbents like CallRail have moved upmarket to serve larger customers with complex needs (conversation intelligence, multi-channel attribution), leaving a gap for a simple, low-cost solution for small operators.
- Regulatory / platform risk: High dependence on Google's ecosystem. Changes to Google Business Profile could impact the value proposition.
