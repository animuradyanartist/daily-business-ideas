# Market: Small Non-Profit Operational Software (US)
slug: small-non-profit-operational-software

## 2026-06-13 revision — idea: The Small Non-Profit's Grant Compliance & Reporting Hub (score: 95)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM: ~$2.6B global grant management software market. SAM: ~200,000 US non-profits ($50k-$5M budget) representing a ~$190M addressable market for a compliance tool. SOM (Y3): ~$1.9M ARR at 1% penetration.
- Growth: The overall market is growing at ~10% CAGR, driven by increased competition for funding and a push for greater accountability.
- Fragmentation: Highly fragmented. Hundreds of thousands of small non-profit buyers. The top 5 software vendors hold only 23% of the market.
- Margin pool: Sits with focused SaaS providers who can deliver high-value, affordable tools. Incumbent complexity and pricing create a large margin umbrella.
- Distribution control: Decentralized. No single gatekeeper. Access is through content marketing, SEO, and partnerships with numerous state and national non-profit associations.
- Incumbents and inertia: Incumbents like Blackbaud and Salesforce are too expensive and complex for this segment. The real competitor is the inertia of "good enough" spreadsheets, which a purpose-built, risk-mitigating tool can overcome.
- Regulatory / platform risk: Low. Grant reporting requirements are set by thousands of individual funders, not a single platform or regulatory body.
