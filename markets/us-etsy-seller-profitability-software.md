# Market: US Etsy Seller Profitability Software
slug: us-etsy-seller-profitability-software

## 2026-07-30 revision — idea: The Etsy seller's "true profit" dashboard (score: 79)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM: $387B (Creator Economy). SAM: 560k professional sellers x $348/yr = $194.8M. SOM: 1,120 sellers x $348/yr = $390k.
- Growth: Etsy's seller base growth has normalized post-pandemic, but the creator economy market is growing at a CAGR of over 23%.
- Fragmentation: Extremely fragmented. Millions of solo operators and micro-businesses.
- Margin pool: Sits with the sellers. This tool helps them protect and increase their margin by providing accurate cost data for better pricing decisions.
- Distribution control: Etsy controls the platform and API access. Third-party developers are dependent on their terms of service. Customer acquisition happens in open communities (Reddit, Facebook, Pinterest).
- Incumbents and inertia: The main incumbents are manual spreadsheets and all-in-one tools (Alura, Everbee) where profit calculation is a secondary feature. The inertia of a "good enough" free spreadsheet is the primary barrier to adoption.
- Regulatory / platform risk: High platform risk. Etsy could change API access or launch a first-party tool, rendering this obsolete.
