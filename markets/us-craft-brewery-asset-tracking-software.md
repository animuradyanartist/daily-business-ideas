# Market: US Craft Brewery Asset Tracking Software
slug: us-craft-brewery-asset-tracking-software

## 2026-08-21 revision — idea: The small brewery's "find my kegs" tracker (score: 95)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM: $60M-$100M annual keg loss value. SAM: ~5,600 breweries x $1.2k/yr = $6.7M. SOM (Yr 3): 168 breweries x $1.2k/yr = $200k ARR. (Sources: Brewers Association)
- Growth: Market contracting (-2.9% in 2025), but operational efficiency pressure is increasing. (Source: Brewers Association)
- Fragmentation: Highly fragmented buyer base (~9,300 breweries), with no single brewer having significant market share.
- Margin pool: Margin sits with the software provider who can prevent direct capital loss for the brewery, capturing a fraction of the savings created.
- Distribution control: Distribution is direct via online marketing, industry forums, and trade groups. No gatekeepers.
- Incumbents and inertia: Incumbents are large, all-in-one platforms (Ekos) or smaller, first-gen tracking apps (Kegshoe). The primary inertia is the "good enough" spreadsheet. The gap is for a modern, standalone, UX-focused tool.
- Regulatory / platform risk: None. This is a pure operational efficiency tool.
