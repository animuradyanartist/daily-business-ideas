## 2026-08-06 revision — idea: The independent used car dealer's "recon-to-retail" tracker (score: 98)

- Growth: Market growing at 10.8% CAGR.
- Fragmentation: Highly fragmented buyer side with ~53,000 active independent dealers.
- Margin pool: Dealers' gross profit is directly tied to inventory turn and minimizing holding costs ($40-$85/day/car). This software directly addresses protecting that margin.
- Distribution control: No single channel controls access. Reachable via targeted digital ads, content marketing, and presence in online dealer communities.
- Incumbents and inertia: Incumbents like Rapid Recon are expensive and complex, creating a gap for a simple, affordable solution. DMS modules are often not best-in-class, creating a gap for a superior point solution.
- Regulatory / platform risk: Low. No specific regulations govern this internal workflow software. No platform risk as it can operate standalone.
