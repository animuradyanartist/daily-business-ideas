# Market: Private Security SMB Operational Tools
slug: private-security-smb-operational-tools

## 2026-06-09 revision — idea: The "Proof of Patrol" & Guard Welfare System for Security Companies (score: 87)
- **TAM/SAM/SOM:** TAM: 8,000 US firms x $2k/yr = $16M. SAM: 5,600 SMB firms x $1.2k/yr = $6.72M. SOM (Y2): 112 firms x $1.2k/yr = $134k. (Sources: IBISWorld, GlobeNewswire)
- **Growth:** Global market growing at 3-9% CAGR, driven by increasing security concerns and outsourcing trends. (Sources: Grand View Research, Valuates Reports)
- **Fragmentation:** Highly fragmented. A few dominant players (e.g., Allied Universal) but over 8,000 firms in the US, creating a long tail of underserved SMBs.
- **Margin pool:** Margin sits with the security service provider. Their ability to retain clients via proof-of-service and mitigate liability directly protects their 6-15% net profit margins.
- **Distribution control:** No single channel owner. Distribution is accessible via targeted content marketing (SEO), direct outreach (LinkedIn), and industry communities.
- **Incumbents and inertia:** Incumbents (TrackTik, GuardsPro) offer complex, expensive, all-in-one suites. They have moved upmarket, leaving a gap for a simple, affordable tool focused on the most acute pains of the SMB segment.
- **Regulatory / platform risk:** Low. OSHA's lone worker guidelines are a tailwind, not a risk. No platform dependency.
