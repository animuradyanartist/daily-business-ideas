# Market: US Independent Medical Courier Compliance Software
slug: us-independent-medical-courier-compliance-software

## 2026-07-31 revision — idea: The independent medical courier's "chain of custody" evidence locker (score: 98)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM: $7.6B (Global Medical Courier Market). SAM: $30M (5,000 US SMB couriers x $600/yr). SOM: $300k (250 firms x $1,200/yr). (Sources: GlobeNewswire, internal estimates)
- Growth: Market growing at 4.3% CAGR, driven by increased diagnostic testing and home healthcare. (Source: GlobeNewswire)
- Fragmentation: Highly fragmented. A few national players (MedSpeed) and a long tail of thousands of local/regional independent operators.
- Margin pool: Margin sits with the courier who can secure and retain high-value, recurring contracts with large healthcare providers. Software that enables this is a high-leverage spend.
- Distribution control: Customer access is controlled by the large labs and hospitals. Couriers who meet their compliance demands get access to the best contracts.
- Incumbents and inertia: Incumbents are horizontal logistics platforms (Onfleet) that are too expensive/complex for the long tail. Inertia is the use of paper logs, which is being rendered obsolete by client demands.
- Regulatory / platform risk: High regulatory component (HIPAA, CLIA) which is a driver, not a risk. Low platform risk as it's an independent B2B tool.
