# Market: US Pawn Shop Compliance Software
slug: us-pawn-shop-compliance-software

## 2026-09-20 revision — idea: The Pawn Shop's "Never-Fail" Daily Police Report Generator (score: 100)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM: $15.3M (8,516 shops x $1,800/yr). SAM: $9.7M (5,386 independent shops x $1,800/yr). SOM (Y3): $486k. Method based on industry data and comparable pricing.
- Growth: Industry is declining slowly (~1%/year), but the need for compliance is constant.
- Fragmentation: Highly fragmented. 63% of shops are independent operators. Two large public companies (EZCorp, FirstCash) control about a third of locations.
- Margin pool: Margin sits with the all-in-one POS providers who bundle compliance reporting with their core software.
- Distribution control: Controlled by incumbent POS vendors and industry associations. A forced-migration event (BWI shutdown) temporarily disrupts this control.
- Incumbents and inertia: Incumbents are large, complex POS systems. Inertia is high, but the BWI shutdown forces an active re-evaluation, leaving them vulnerable to a focused, best-in-class utility.
- Regulatory / platform risk: High regulatory component, but this is a feature. Laws vary by state/municipality, requiring ongoing maintenance. Platform risk is low as reporting destinations (like LeadsOnline) have an incentive to accept data from multiple sources.
