# Market: US Part 135 Air Charter Operator Compliance Software
slug: us-part-135-air-charter-compliance-software

## 2026-07-21 revision — idea: The Part 135 Air Charter Operator's "FAA Ramp Check-Ready" Digital Binder (score: 98)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM: Global Aviation Compliance Software (Billions). SAM: ~800 US operators × $3,588/year = ~$2.87M. SOM (Yr 3): 40 operators × $3,588/year = ~$143k ARR.
- Growth: Market growing steadily with business aviation flight hours. Upcoming 2027 SMS mandate creates a near-term catalyst.
- Fragmentation: Highly fragmented on the operator side (>1,000 operators), consolidated on the regulatory side (FAA).
- Margin pool: Margin sits with the operators who can maintain compliance and operational efficiency. Software that reduces existential risk has high pricing power.
- Distribution control: The FAA controls the regulations and publishes the list of all potential customers, enabling direct access.
- Incumbents and inertia: Incumbents are either too complex and expensive (Web Manuals), too broad (FlyAXS), or are generalists (FileFlo). The inertia is operators using paper/spreadsheets, which is becoming untenable.
- Regulatory / platform risk: Low. The underlying FAA regulations are stable and slow to change. This is not a platform-risk business like Amazon FBA.
