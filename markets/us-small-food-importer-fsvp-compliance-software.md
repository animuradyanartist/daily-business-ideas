# Market: US Small Food Importer FSVP Compliance Software
slug: us-small-food-importer-fsvp-compliance-software

## 2026-08-05 revision — idea: The small food importer's "FSVP-ready" compliance binder (score: 98)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM: $210B+ US food import market. SAM: ~20,000 small importers x $1,188/yr = $23.76M. SOM (Y3): 200 importers x $1,188/yr = $237k ARR.
- Growth: US food imports are a large, stable, and growing category. FDA enforcement is actively increasing.
- Fragmentation: Buyer side is highly fragmented (thousands of small importers). Supplier side (of compliance services) is concentrated among a few large firms and a long tail of small consultants.
- Margin pool: Margin sits with the consultants and service agents who charge high fees for manual document preparation and review. This can be captured by software.
- Distribution control: Customer access is currently controlled by Google Search (buyers in pain) and customs brokers (who advise clients).
- Incumbents and inertia: Incumbents are service-based businesses (Registrar Corp, etc.). Their business model is predicated on high-touch, high-cost manual service. They have no incentive to build a low-cost, self-serve tool that would cannibalize their core revenue.
- Regulatory / platform risk: Low. The business is built on a foundational FDA rule (FSVP) that is a core part of FSMA and is unlikely to be repealed.
- Key sources: https://www.registrarcorp.com, https://www.fda.gov/food/food-safety-modernization-act-fsma/fsma-final-rule-foreign-supplier-verification-programs-fsvp-importers-food-humans-and-animals, https://www.reddit.com/r/CustomsBroker/
