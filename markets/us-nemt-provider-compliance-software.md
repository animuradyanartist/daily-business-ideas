# Market: US NEMT Provider Compliance Software
slug: us-nemt-provider-compliance-software

## 2026-07-16 revision — idea: The NEMT provider's "audit-proof trip log" to get paid by Medicaid (score: 98)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM ~$1.15B (NEMT Software, North America), SAM ~$56.6M (Small/Medium US Providers), SOM (Yr 3) ~$1.13M. Method: TAM from market reports, SAM based on estimated provider count and realistic pricing, SOM is 2% of SAM.
- Growth: 5-9% CAGR, driven by aging population and prevalence of chronic disease.
- Fragmentation: Provider side is highly fragmented (thousands of small fleets). Payer/Broker side is highly concentrated (Modivcare, MTM, etc.).
- Margin pool: Sits with the brokers and MCOs who control reimbursement. Providers operate on thin margins and are highly sensitive to revenue loss from denied claims.
- Distribution control: Brokers control patient access. Direct distribution to providers via targeted digital ads and content marketing is the most viable channel.
- Incumbents and inertia: Incumbents (RouteGenie, TripMaster) are full-suite, complex systems. They leave a gap for a simple, focused tool that solves the single most acute pain point (claim denials) with a superior user experience.
- Regulatory / platform risk: High dependence on Medicaid policy and the power of a few national brokers. A change in reimbursement rules or a broker offering a free competing tool is the primary risk.
