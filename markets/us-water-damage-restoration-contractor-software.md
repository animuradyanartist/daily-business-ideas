# Market: US Water Damage Restoration Contractor Software
slug: us-water-damage-restoration-contractor-software

## 2026-07-28 revision — idea: The water damage restoration contractor's "get paid by insurance" evidence locker (score: 98)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM: $7.1B (US Damage Restoration market). SAM: $100.3M (42k firms x $2,388/yr). SOM (Yr 2): $501k (210 firms). Sources: IBISWorld.
- Growth: Stable growth, correlated with increase in severe weather events and aging infrastructure.
- Fragmentation: Highly fragmented buyer market with over 60,000 businesses, most operating locally. No single buyer has pricing power.
- Margin pool: Margin sits with the contractors, but it is constantly under threat from the ultimate payers: a concentrated group of large insurance carriers who seek to minimize claim payouts.
- Distribution control: Customer access is gained through channels that build trust with contractors: industry training bodies (IICRC), trade publications (Cleanfax), and SEO for specific, pain-aware keywords.
- Incumbents and inertia: Incumbents are either complex, all-in-one platforms (Encircle) or horizontal tools that don't solve the specific compliance workflow (CompanyCam, Jobber). The gap exists for a simple, mobile-first tool focused solely on creating a defensible, "adjuster-proof" report.
- Regulatory / platform risk: Low regulatory risk. The primary platform risk is the power of the insurance carriers to change documentation standards, which is also the primary driver of demand.
