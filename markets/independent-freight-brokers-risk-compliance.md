# Market: Independent Freight Brokers (Risk & Compliance Tools)
slug: independent-freight-brokers-risk-compliance

## 2026-06-05 revision — idea: The Independent Freight Broker's "Carrier Vetting & Compliance" Hub (score: 84)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM: $20.64B (U.S. freight brokerage market 2026). SAM: ~26,000 independent U.S. freight brokerages. SOM (2-yr): 130 customers for ~$201k ARR.
- Growth: 8.6% CAGR for the U.S. market.
- Fragmentation: Highly fragmented buyer side (26,000+ small brokers), concentrated supplier side for data (FMCSA, a few major data providers).
- Margin pool: The broker's 10-20% gross margin per load, which is at risk from fraud and liability claims.
- Distribution control: Fragmented. No single channel controls access to brokers. Dominated by content (industry publications) and communities (Reddit, LinkedIn).
- Incumbents and inertia: Incumbents like Carrier411 provide raw data but lack a modern workflow layer. Inertia is the manual, ad-hoc vetting process, which is now legally risky.
- Regulatory / platform risk: Low. The core data source (FMCSA) is a public government entity. Recent legal rulings increase the need for this product.
