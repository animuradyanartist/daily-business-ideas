# Market: Academic Research Lab Operational Software (US)
slug: academic-research-lab-operational-software

## 2026-06-12 revision — idea: The Academic Research Lab's "Shared Equipment" Scheduler (score: 89)
- **TAM/SAM/SOM:** TAM ~$47.4M for schedulers in R1/R2 US universities. Broader lab management market is $6.2B globally. Method: (# R1/R2 Universities * est. # labs/uni) * ARR. Sources: Carnegie Classification, Grand View Research.
- **Growth:** 9.9% CAGR for the broader lab management market. Source: Grand View Research.
- **Fragmentation:** Highly fragmented buyer base. Tens of thousands of individual labs, each with discretionary grant funding, making independent purchasing decisions.
- **Margin pool:** High software margins (>90%). Value is in saving time for high-salaried researchers and preventing costly equipment damage.
- **Distribution control:** No single channel controls access. Distribution is achieved through content marketing, SEO, and direct outreach to publicly listed PIs and labs.
- **Incumbents and inertia:** Incumbents (Agilent, BookitLab) focus on top-down, enterprise sales to centralized "core facilities." This leaves the long tail of individual labs, who default to using free, inadequate tools (Google Calendar), open for a bottom-up, product-led solution.
- **Regulatory / platform risk:** Low. No specific regulations for scheduling software. No platform dependency.
