# Market: U.S. NCLEX Repeat-Taker Test Prep Software
slug: us-nclex-repeat-taker-test-prep

## 2026-08-12 revision — idea: The NCLEX repeat-taker's "pass-the-second-time" study system (score: 98)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM: Global Test Prep ($40B+). SAM: ~42.5k U.S. RN repeat takers/yr × $199 = ~$8.46M/yr. SOM (Yr 3): 2.1k candidates = ~$423k ARR.
- Growth: Stable, recurring cohort of failing candidates each year, tied to the overall growth in the nursing profession.
- Fragmentation: Buyer base is highly fragmented (individuals). Supplier side is concentrated among a few large players (UWorld, Kaplan, Archer).
- Margin pool: Value is in unlocking a ~$97.5k/yr starting salary. The margin sits with the solution that can most credibly de-risk the exam and activate that career.
- Distribution control: Controlled by brand recognition and search rankings for "NCLEX prep," and community trust in forums like Reddit and allnurses.com.
- Incumbents and inertia: Incumbents (UWorld, Kaplan) are optimized for the larger first-time taker market. They have not built a differentiated, diagnostic-first product for repeaters, leaving a clear strategic gap.
- Regulatory / platform risk: Low. The NCLEX exam format is public, and the Candidate Performance Report is a standard document provided to all failing candidates.
