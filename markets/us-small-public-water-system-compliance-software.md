# Market: US Small Public Water System Compliance Software
slug: us-small-public-water-system-compliance-software

## 2026-09-16 revision — idea: The Small Water Treatment Plant's "State-Ready" Monthly Operating Report (MOR) Generator (score: 100)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM: Global water management software market ($B's). SAM: ~18,300 US water systems serving 501-10,000 people x $3,600/yr = ~$66M ARR. SOM (Yr 3): Capture 1.5% = ~$1M ARR.
- Growth: Stable to slow growth in number of systems, but strong growth in the need for digital tools driven by workforce turnover and increasing regulatory pressure.
- Fragmentation: Highly fragmented buyer base. 97% of all public water systems serve <10,000 people, making them unattractive to large enterprise vendors.
- Margin pool: Sits with the software provider who can de-risk the operator's personal license and save high-cost labor hours spent on manual reporting.
- Distribution control: State Rural Water Associations are the key trust and distribution channel.
- Incumbents and inertia: The primary incumbent is Microsoft Excel. Existing software vendors are either too complex/expensive for small systems or are trying to be all-in-one platforms, leaving a gap for a best-in-class point solution for compliance reporting.
- Regulatory / platform risk: Low. The underlying need is mandated by state and federal law (Safe Drinking Water Act).
