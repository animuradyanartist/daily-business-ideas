# Market: US Skilled Trades Apprentice Licensing Software
slug: us-skilled-trades-apprentice-licensing-software

## 2026-08-22 revision — idea: The Apprentice Electrician's "Get-My-License" Hour & Category Tracker (score: 92)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM: US Skilled Trades Industry. SAM: ~324,000 electrical apprentices in the US at any time. SOM (Year 3): ~3,240 customers. Method: BLS job openings x 4-year apprenticeship duration. Sources: BLS.gov.
- Growth: Electrician jobs projected to grow 6% from 2022-2032, ensuring a steady inflow of new apprentices. Source: BLS.gov.
- Fragmentation: Highly fragmented. Hundreds of thousands of individual apprentices working for thousands of small to medium-sized contractors.
- Margin pool: The value sits with the apprentice, who stands to gain a ~$20,000/year pay increase upon licensure. The software's value is in de-risking and accelerating that outcome.
- Distribution control: No single entity controls access. Distribution is via open channels like social media, search engines, and app stores.
- Incumbents and inertia: The primary incumbent is the manual spreadsheet or paper logbook. Inertia is high, but the clear value proposition can overcome it. Existing apps are not professional-grade, leaving a gap at the premium end.
- Regulatory / platform risk: Low. The business benefits from state-level regulation. Risk of being delisted from app stores is minimal.
- Key sources: https://www.bls.gov/ooh/construction-and-extraction/electricians.htm, https://www.reddit.com/r/electricians/, https://coradine.com/logten/pricing/
