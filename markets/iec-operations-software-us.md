# Market: Independent Educational Consultant (IEC) Operations Software (US)
slug: iec-operations-software-us

## 2026-06-25 revision — idea: The Independent College Admissions Consultant's "Application Tracker" (score: 98)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM: $7.2B (Global College Admissions Consulting Market, 2025). SAM: $7.2M/yr (6,000 full-time US IECs x $1,200/yr). SOM (Yr 2): $204k.
- Growth: Market is growing steadily. US market grew at 3.3% CAGR to 2025; global market projected at 5.7%-6.8% CAGR through 2029. The number of practitioners has more than doubled since 2007.
- Fragmentation: Highly fragmented buyer base of thousands of solo and small-firm consultants.
- Margin pool: Margin sits with the software provider. The high value of the consultant's service ($4k-$7k packages) makes a ~$100/mo software fee a low-friction operational expense.
- Distribution control: Controlled by incumbents with existing user bases and presence in professional communities (IECA, etc.). A new entrant must win on product and targeted content marketing.
- Incumbents and inertia: Incumbents like CollegePlannerPro and CounselMore are established but can be complex. Inertia is the primary barrier, but the growing number of new IECs provides a constant stream of uncommitted customers.
- Regulatory / platform risk: Low. The college application process is standardized (e.g., Common App) but not controlled by a single API-gated platform.
