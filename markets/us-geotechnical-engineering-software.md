# Market: US Geotechnical Engineering Software
slug: us-geotechnical-engineering-software

## 2026-09-14 revision — idea: The Independent Geotechnical Engineer's "gINT-Replacement" Boring Log Hub (score: 100)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM: $1.56B (global market). SAM: $10.8M (US small/mid-size firms). SOM: $108k (Year 1 target).
- Growth: 11.83% CAGR, driven by infrastructure spending.
- Fragmentation: Highly fragmented buyer side with thousands of small-to-mid-sized engineering firms.
- Margin pool: Sits with the software vendor; >90% gross margins are typical for vertical SaaS.
- Distribution control: Historically controlled by Bentley (gINT). Their sunsetting of the product has decentralized customer access, creating an opening for competitors via content marketing and direct outreach.
- Incumbents and inertia: Bentley is the primary incumbent. They are creating an opportunity by forcing users off a legacy product (gINT) onto a new, unpopular one (OpenGround), creating a market-wide evaluation trigger.
- Regulatory / platform risk: Low. The primary risk is market risk from competitors, not regulatory or platform dependency.
