# Market: US Independent Restaurant Compliance Software
slug: us-independent-restaurant-compliance-software

## 2026-08-02 revision — idea: The restaurant manager's "health-inspection-ready" food safety log (score: 98)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM: $1.55T (total industry sales). SAM: ~412,000 independent restaurants x $1,188/yr = $489.5M. SOM (Yr 2): 412 restaurants x $1,188/yr = ~$490k ARR.
- Growth: The number of independent restaurants is shrinking (-2.3% in 2025), but the pressure to professionalize and adopt technology is increasing for survivors. The overall digital food safety software market is growing.
- Fragmentation: Buyer market is highly fragmented (7 in 10 restaurants are single-unit operations), giving individual buyers low power.
- Margin pool: Margin sits with the software provider. Restaurants operate on thin 3-5% net margins and will pay to protect revenue from high-consequence events like inspection failures.
- Distribution control: No single entity controls distribution. Reachable through digital ads, content marketing, and direct outreach.
- Incumbents and inertia: Incumbent is pen-and-paper. Large POS platforms are not focused on deep compliance workflows. Existing digital players often target larger, more complex enterprises, leaving a gap for a simple, affordable solution for independents.
- Regulatory / platform risk: The forcing function is local and state health regulation, which is stable and unlikely to decrease in stringency. Low platform risk.
