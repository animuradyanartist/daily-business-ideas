# Market: Self-Managed HOA Compliance Tools
slug: self-managed-hoa-compliance-tools

## 2026-06-07 revision — idea: The "Know Your Rights & Rules" Hub for Self-Managed HOAs (score: 86)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM: ~$185.5M (371k HOAs * $500). SAM: ~$64.9M (130k self-managed HOAs * $499). SOM: ~$1.3M (2% of SAM). Sources: Fortune Business Insights, HOPB.
- Growth: Market growing steadily with 3k-10k new HOAs formed annually. Source: HOPB.
- Fragmentation: Highly fragmented. ~130,000 self-managed HOAs act as independent buyers. No channel concentration.
- Margin pool: Margin sits with whoever can provide authoritative, risk-reducing information efficiently. Currently held by expensive lawyers.
- Distribution control: No one controls distribution. Open field for SEO and content marketing.
- Incumbents and inertia: High-end incumbents (AppFolio) are too complex/expensive. Low-end (PayHOA) are not focused on the legal compliance job-to-be-done. Inertia is using free, unreliable Google search results.
- Regulatory / platform risk: High. Product's value depends on maintaining accuracy with constantly changing state laws. This is both a risk and the moat.
- Key sources: https://www.hopb.co/blog/self-managed-hoa, https://www.fortunebusinessinsights.com/hoa-software-market-109151, https://www.payhoa.com/pricing
