# Market: US Small Business ERISA (Form 5500) Compliance Software
slug: us-small-business-erisa-compliance-software

## 2026-09-01 revision — idea: The Small Business's "Penalty-Proof" Form 5500 Filer (score: 99)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM: Multi-billion US benefits compliance market. SAM: ~720,000 small businesses x $500/yr = ~$360M. SOM (Yr 3): 1,800 businesses x $500/yr = $900k ARR. (Sources: Gusto, U.S. Census)
- Growth: Positive. Small business retirement plan adoption grew 64% from 2019-2026, driven by tight labor markets and state mandates. (Source: PLANSPONSOR, Gusto)
- Fragmentation: Highly fragmented buyer base of millions of small businesses across all industries. Ideal for self-serve SaaS.
- Margin pool: Value is captured by preventing catastrophic government penalties ($150,000+), making the software a high-ROI insurance policy.
- Distribution control: No single entity controls distribution. Access is via search (SEO, SEM) and partnerships with accountants/payroll providers.
- Incumbents and inertia: Incumbents are high-cost service providers (TPAs) or complex professional software. Inertia is high due to fear of making a mistake, but this is overcome by a massive price discount and superior user experience.
- Regulatory / platform risk: High. The business is entirely dependent on the existence and structure of Form 5500. A change in filing requirements by the DOL/IRS could fundamentally alter the market.
