# Market: Shopify Merchants (Profitability Tools)
slug: shopify-merchants-profit-tools

## 2026-06-02 revision — idea: The Shopify Merchant's Real Profit Dashboard (score: 81)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM: Global e-commerce ($378B+ Shopify GMV). SAM: 2.5M+ actively trading Shopify stores. SOM: A new app can target 1,000 stores in Year 1 ($348k ARR).
- Growth: Shopify's GMV and merchant base are growing faster than the overall e-commerce market.
- Fragmentation: Buyer side is highly fragmented (millions of SMBs). Supplier side (app developers) is also fragmented but consolidating around a few leaders per category.
- Margin pool: Sits with the app developers who can provide high-value automation and insights via a monthly SaaS subscription. Shopify takes a 15% commission.
- Distribution control: The Shopify App Store has near-total control over customer access, creating a centralized but competitive channel.
- Incumbents and inertia: Incumbents are feature-rich but can be complex and expensive, leaving a gap for a simpler, more focused, and better-designed solution.
- Regulatory / platform risk: High platform risk. Shopify could build a competing feature or change App Store rules at any time.
