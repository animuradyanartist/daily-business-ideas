# Market: Home Health Agency Compliance & Operations Software (US)
slug: home-health-agency-compliance-software-us

## 2026-06-21 revision — idea: The Home Healthcare Agency's "Audit-Proof" EVV & Scheduling Hub (score: 96)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM: $61-258B (US Home Healthcare Market). SAM: $35.8M (10k small agencies x $299/mo). SOM (Y2): $837k (200 agencies x $349/mo).
- Growth: 8-12% CAGR, driven by aging population and preference for at-home care.
- Fragmentation: Highly fragmented. ~11,000-33,000 agencies in the US, many are small, independent businesses.
- Margin pool: Sits with the software that enables compliant billing and operational efficiency, as this directly protects the agency's thin profit margins from being erased by claim denials and administrative overhead.
- Distribution control: No single entity controls distribution. Direct B2B marketing, content marketing, and partnerships with state-level industry associations are effective channels.
- Incumbents and inertia: Incumbents (WellSky, Axxess) are large, complex, all-in-one EMRs. Their inertia and complexity create a gap for a simple, unbundled point solution that solves the most acute pain (EVV compliance & scheduling) for smaller agencies that are overwhelmed by the incumbents' feature sets and price points.
- Regulatory / platform risk: High. The business is predicated on HIPAA compliance and aligning with the 21st Century Cures Act EVV mandate. A failure in security or compliance would be existential.
