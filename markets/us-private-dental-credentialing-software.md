# Market: US Private Dental Practice Credentialing Software
slug: us-private-dental-credentialing-software

## 2026-09-03 revision — idea: The Dental Practice's "Get Paid on Day One" Credentialing Hub (score: 100)
- **TAM/SAM/SOM:** TAM: $196B US Dental Market. SAM: ~$441M (147k practices * 20% annual need * $1.5k/deal). SOM (Y2): $450k. Sources: IBISWorld, ADA.
- **Growth:** Stable at ~1.1% per year. Pressure from DSOs is increasing the need for operational efficiency in private practices. Source: ADA.
- **Fragmentation:** Highly fragmented. 73% of dentists own their practice, and many are solo practitioners. This makes the market inaccessible to high-touch enterprise sales.
- **Margin pool:** Sits with the dental practice. Every day a credentialed provider can bill insurance is pure margin improvement. The software captures a fraction of this unlocked revenue.
- **Distribution control:** No single entity controls distribution. Access is via direct-to-practitioner digital marketing (search, social) and content.
- **Incumbents and inertia:** Incumbents are slow, expensive manual service providers. Inertia is the "we've always done it this way" mentality of using the office manager. The gap is a fast, cheap, self-serve tool that provides transparency.
- **Regulatory / platform risk:** HIPAA compliance is a major, non-negotiable requirement. A data breach would be fatal. No significant platform risk.
