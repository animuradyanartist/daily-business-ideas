# Market: 3PL Food Safety Compliance Software (US)
slug: 3pl-food-safety-compliance-software-us

## 2026-06-20 revision — idea: The 3PL's "Audit-Proof" Food Safety Compliance Hub (score: 96)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM: >$270B US 3PL Market. SAM: ~$65.4M (18.2k facilities x $3,588/yr). SOM: ~$326k (91 facilities). Sources: IBISWorld, GlobeNewswire.
- Growth: 7.7% CAGR for the overall 3PL market, but the key driver is increasing regulatory enforcement and customer pressure. Source: GlobeNewswire.
- Fragmentation: Highly fragmented market of tens of thousands of small, independent 3PLs. Low buyer concentration.
- Margin pool: Margin sits with the software provider who can mitigate a high-cost, business-ending risk for a low monthly fee relative to the potential loss.
- Distribution control: No single entity controls access. Distribution is possible via direct outreach, content marketing (SEO), and targeted digital ads.
- Incumbents and inertia: Incumbents are large, complex, and expensive WMS or enterprise food safety platforms. Their inertia and focus on large customers leave a clear gap for a simple, affordable tool for the fragmented SMB segment.
- Regulatory / platform risk: Low. The business is built on top of a stable federal regulation (FSMA). The risk is the regulation being repealed or enforcement declining, which is highly unlikely.
