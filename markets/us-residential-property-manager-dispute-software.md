# Market: US Residential Property Manager Dispute Software
slug: us-residential-property-manager-dispute-software

## 2026-08-01 revision — idea: The property manager's "security deposit dispute" evidence locker (score: 98)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM: $139.9B (US Property Management Market). SAM: ~$144M (245k residential PMs x $588/yr). SOM (Yr 3): ~$721k ARR.
- Growth: Core market is slow (1-4%), but software sub-segment is fast (6-10% CAGR).
- Fragmentation: Highly fragmented. Over 245,000 residential property management businesses in the US.
- Margin pool: Value is in preventing loss of security deposits ($700-$2000+) and avoiding punitive damages (2-3x deposit) and saving dozens of hours of staff time per dispute.
- Distribution control: No single gatekeeper. Access via content marketing, online communities (BiggerPockets, Reddit), and eventually PMS app marketplaces.
- Incumbents and inertia: Large PMS platforms (AppFolio, Buildium) have generic inspection modules. Standalone tools (zInspector) focus on operational efficiency, not legal defensibility. The gap is the purpose-built "dispute-proof" workflow.
- Regulatory / platform risk: High. Landlord-tenant law varies significantly by state and is generally trending pro-tenant, which increases the value of a compliance/documentation tool.
