# Market: US SMB CRO & Biotech eTMF Software
slug: us-smb-cro-biotech-etmf-software

## 2026-07-18 revision — idea: The small CRO's "FDA audit-ready" eTMF (score: 98)
- **TAM/SAM/SOM:** Global eTMF market ~$1.4B in 2025 (TAM). US market for SMBs (4,500 CROs + ~1,000 biotechs) at $25k ACV = ~$137.5M (SAM). Year 2 SOM of $500k is realistic.
- **Growth:** Strong ~13-14% CAGR, driven by increasing number/complexity of trials and outsourcing to CROs.
- **Fragmentation:** Buyer side is highly fragmented (thousands of small CROs/biotechs). Supplier side is concentrated at the high end (Veeva).
- **Margin pool:** Sits with the software vendor who can successfully sell a high-LTV subscription based on the catastrophic cost of non-compliance.
- **Distribution control:** Incumbents control via enterprise sales teams. Challengers can win with targeted digital marketing and content that speaks to the pain of SMBs priced out by incumbents.
- **Incumbents and inertia:** Veeva is the dominant incumbent but is expensive, complex, and slow to implement, creating a classic price/simplicity gap for a challenger to attack at the low end of the market.
- **Regulatory / platform risk:** High. The product *must* be 21 CFR Part 11 compliant and validated. Failure here is an existential threat to the business.
