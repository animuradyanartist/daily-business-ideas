# Market: US Small Machine Shop Job Costing Software
slug: us-small-machine-shop-job-costing-software

## 2026-08-23 revision — idea: The Small Machine Shop's "Real-Time Job Profitability" Tracker (score: 96)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM: $44.7B US market. SAM: 17,100 shops x $1,800/yr = $30.7M/yr. SOM: 171 shops = $307k/yr.
- Growth: Stable, tied to overall manufacturing activity which is currently strong.
- Fragmentation: Highly fragmented buyer base; no single shop has significant market share.
- Margin pool: Sits with the shop owner, who directly benefits from improved job profitability.
- Distribution control: No gatekeepers. Reachable via open communities (forums, YouTube) and direct marketing.
- Incumbents and inertia: Incumbents (ECI, ProShop) are too complex and expensive for the small shop segment. Inertia is reliance on manual spreadsheets.
- Regulatory / platform risk: Low. No dependence on a single platform or regulation.
