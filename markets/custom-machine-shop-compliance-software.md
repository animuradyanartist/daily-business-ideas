# Market: Custom Machine Shop Compliance Software (US)
slug: custom-machine-shop-compliance-software

## 2026-06-16 revision — idea: The Custom Job Shop's "Audit-Proof" Material Traceability Log (score: 92)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM: $1.31B (Job Shop Software, 2025). SAM: $9.17M (US shops needing traceability). SOM: $91k (1% of SAM).
- Growth: Underlying market growing at 9.5% CAGR. Demand driven by strong aerospace/defense sectors.
- Fragmentation: Highly fragmented buyer base of ~17,100 small shops. No single buyer has pricing power.
- Margin pool: Margin sits with the software provider that can successfully mitigate the high cost of compliance failure for these shops.
- Distribution control: No single entity controls distribution. Direct access to buyers is possible via targeted digital marketing and content.
- Incumbents and inertia: Incumbents are large, complex, expensive ERPs (ProShop, JobBOSS²). Their inertia and high price points create a significant gap for an affordable, user-friendly, unbundled solution.
- Regulatory / platform risk: Low. The need is driven by established industry standards (AS9100) mandated by customers, not a volatile government regulation. No platform risk.
