# Market: Independent Auto Repair Shop Software (US)
slug: independent-auto-repair-shop-software

## 2026-06-11 revision — idea: The Independent Auto Repair Shop's "Parts Procurement" Hub (score: 91)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM: $3.4B (Global Auto Repair Software, 2026). SAM: $202.5M (170.5k US independent shops x $99/mo). SOM: $1M ARR (850 shops).
- Growth: Market growing at ~14% CAGR, driven by vehicle complexity and digitization.
- Fragmentation: Extremely fragmented buyer base. 75% of 227k+ US shops are single-location independents. No single customer has pricing power.
- Margin pool: Margin sits with the software provider that can deliver the most direct ROI in technician/advisor efficiency. Shops pay for tools that increase throughput and parts profit.
- Distribution control: No single player controls distribution. Channels include direct sales, content marketing, and integrations with complementary software (e.g., Shop Management Systems).
- Incumbents and inertia: Incumbents are either all-in-one SMS platforms (Tekmetric, Shop-Ware) where parts ordering is a "good enough" feature, or a direct competitor (PartsTech) with a freemium model. Inertia is high, but the pain of inefficiency is a strong catalyst for change.
- Regulatory / platform risk: Low. Some risk from "Right to Repair" legislation, which could alter data access, but this is more likely to benefit independent shops and the tools that serve them.
