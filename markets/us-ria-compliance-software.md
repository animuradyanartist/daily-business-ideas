# Market: US Registered Investment Advisor (RIA) Compliance Software
slug: us-ria-compliance-software

## 2026-09-12 revision — idea: The Registered Investment Advisor's "Audit-Ready" SEC Marketing Rule Archive (score: 100)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM: $15.5B+ Global RegTech. SAM: ~12,000 US RIAs × $1,800 ACV = ~$21.6M. SOM (Yr 2): 240 firms × $1,800 ACV = $432k ARR. (Sources: SEC.gov, industry reports).
- Growth: Strong. RegTech market growing at 22.1% CAGR. SEC enforcement is increasing, driving demand.
- Fragmentation: Highly fragmented buyer market. 16,544 RIAs, average firm has 8 employees. Ideal for product-led growth.
- Margin pool: Sits with software and specialized consulting that can demonstrably reduce the risk of catastrophic SEC fines.
- Distribution control: Controlled by communities (XYPN, Kitces), large platforms (Orion), and compliance consultants who act as trusted advisors.
- Incumbents and inertia: Incumbents are enterprise-focused (Smarsh) and too expensive/complex, or are all-in-one platforms (Orion) where compliance is a secondary feature. Inertia is reliance on manual, non-defensible processes (spreadsheets).
- Regulatory / platform risk: High regulatory dependency; a change in the SEC Marketing Rule could alter the need. Low platform risk, as it integrates with the open web and social media APIs.
