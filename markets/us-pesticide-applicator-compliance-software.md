# Market: US Pesticide Applicator Compliance Software
slug: us-pesticide-applicator-compliance-software

## 2026-08-31 revision — idea: The "Audit-Ready" Pesticide Applicator's Log (score: 100)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM: ~1M certified applicators required to keep records. SAM: 300,000 professional applicators x $348/yr = $104.4M. SOM (Yr 3): 1,000 applicators x $348/yr = $348k ARR.
- Growth: Stable, with growth driven by increased state/federal enforcement and modernization of small service businesses.
- Fragmentation: Highly fragmented. Thousands of solo operators and small businesses, making it ideal for a self-serve SaaS model.
- Margin pool: Sits with software that can provide compliance peace-of-mind cheaply. The cost of failure (fines, license loss) is high, making the value of a low-cost tool significant.
- Distribution control: No single gatekeeper. Access is via direct marketing (Google Ads, content marketing) to a fragmented professional audience.
- Incumbents and inertia: Incumbents are full FSM suites (PestPac, GorillaDesk) that are expensive and complex. Inertia is from paper logs. The gap is a simple, cheap, mobile-first tool that is better than paper and cheaper than an FSM suite.
- Regulatory / platform risk: Low. The underlying requirement (FIFRA) is stable federal law. Risk is in keeping up with state-level variations.
