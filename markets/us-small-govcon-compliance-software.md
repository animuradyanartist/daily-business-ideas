# Market: US Small Government Contractor Compliance Software
slug: us-small-govcon-compliance-software

## 2026-10-01 revision — idea: The Small Government Contractor's "DCAA-Proof" Timesheet Hub (score: 95)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM: $793B+ US GovCon market. SAM: $177M annually (65.5k small contractors * 15 users * $15/user/mo). SOM (Yr 3): $1.77M ARR.
- Growth: Market consolidating; total contract dollars to SMBs are up, but the number of SMBs is down ~40% in a decade. Signals a flight to quality.
- Fragmentation: Highly fragmented buyer base of 65,500+ small businesses across all states and industries.
- Margin pool: Value is in mitigating catastrophic audit failure risk (contract termination, debarment). Software is priced as an insurance premium against this risk.
- Distribution control: No single channel controls access. Buyers are found via SEO for compliance terms, targeted ads, and industry communities.
- Incumbents and inertia: Dominated by complex, expensive ERPs (Deltek, Unanet). Their inertia and high price points create a large, underserved market segment of smaller firms.
- Regulatory / platform risk: Low. The DCAA regulations are the platform; they are stable and slow to change, creating a durable need.
