# Market: US Small-Scale Cosmetics MoCRA Compliance Software
slug: us-small-cosmetics-mocra-compliance-software

## 2026-08-03 revision — idea: The small-scale cosmetics maker's "MoCRA-ready" compliance system (score: 98)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM: $107B US Cosmetics Market. SAM: ~10,000 non-exempt small brands × $1,500/year = ~$15M ARR. SOM: 250 brands × $1,500/year = $375k ARR.
- Growth: Market is mature (4.7% CAGR), but the compliance sub-market was created by new regulation in 2022, so growth is driven by regulatory enforcement deadlines.
- Fragmentation: The buyer base is highly fragmented, consisting of thousands of independent "indie brands" selling online.
- Margin pool: Margin sits with the brands themselves. They are paying for compliance as a cost of goods sold to protect their primary revenue stream. This tool reduces that cost vs. manual alternatives.
- Distribution control: Distribution is open via digital channels. Buyers are accessible through content marketing (SEO for MoCRA-related terms) and paid ads targeting e-commerce founders.
- Incumbents and inertia: Incumbents are high-cost service firms (Registrar Corp) or complex enterprise software (BatchMaster). They leave a gap for an affordable, easy-to-use SaaS product for non-expert founders.
- Regulatory / platform risk: The primary risk is the FDA delaying enforcement or finalization of GMP rules, which would reduce the urgency to buy. The regulation itself is now law.
