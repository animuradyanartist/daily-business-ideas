# Market: US SMB EPA Tier II Compliance Software
slug: us-smb-epa-tier-ii-compliance-software

## 2026-09-22 revision — idea: The Small Business "Penalty-Proof" EPA Tier II Report Generator (score: 93)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM: Global EHS Software Market. SAM: ~230,000 US facilities x $499 ACV = $114.7M/yr. SOM (Yr 2): 1,000 facilities x $499 ACV = $499k ARR. (Source: EPA)
- Growth: Static market based on long-standing regulation, but tailwinds from increasing penalties and SMB digitization are forcing more of the TAM into active compliance. (Source: Williams Mullen)
- Fragmentation: Highly fragmented buyer base of ~463,000 potential facilities across hundreds of different industries.
- Margin pool: Margin sits with software providers who can automate the complex, high-risk reporting process at scale, capturing value from both high-cost manual consultants and free-but-risky government software.
- Distribution control: Distribution is open, primarily driven by search engine marketing and content marketing targeting high-intent keywords during the annual "reporting season" (Dec-Mar).
- Incumbents and inertia: Incumbents are high-cost enterprise EHS suites (overkill for SMBs), expensive consultants (unscalable), and the free EPA "Tier2 Submit" tool (clunky, no guidance, high user error rate). This leaves a clear gap for a user-friendly, affordable, purpose-built SaaS tool.
- Regulatory / platform risk: Low. The underlying EPCRA regulation is stable and has been in place for decades. The business is not dependent on any single tech platform.
