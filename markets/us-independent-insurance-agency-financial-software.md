# Market: US Independent Insurance Agency Financial Software
slug: us-independent-insurance-agency-financial-software

## 2026-09-11 revision — idea: The Independent Insurance Agent's "Leakproof" Commission Reconciler (score: 100)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM: Global insurance software market >$15B. SAM: ~31,200 US independent P&C agencies x $1,200/yr = ~$37.4M. SOM (Yr 3): 624 agencies x $1,200/yr = ~$750k ARR. (sources:, dossier)
- Growth: Market consolidating but stable; growth driven by need for tech-enabled efficiency. (sources:,,)
- Fragmentation: Highly fragmented buyer base of ~39,000 independent agencies. (sources:,)
- Margin pool: Sits with the software provider who can automate a manual process that currently leaks 3-5% of an agency's total revenue. (source:)
- Distribution control: Controlled by large AMS vendors (Vertafore, Applied) and industry media (Insurance Journal). Direct access via targeted digital marketing is viable.
- Incumbents and inertia: Incumbents are large, slow, and sell monolithic systems. Their new tools are embedded, not standalone. Inertia is high due to complexity of switching core AMS. This leaves a gap for a best-in-class, AMS-agnostic point solution. (source:,)
- Regulatory / platform risk: Low. This is a financial operations tool, not a regulated compliance product. Low platform risk as an AMS-agnostic tool.
