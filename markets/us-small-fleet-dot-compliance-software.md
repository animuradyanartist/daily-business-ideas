# Market: US Small Fleet DOT Compliance Software
slug: us-small-fleet-dot-compliance-software

## 2026-08-27 revision — idea: The Independent Trucking Company's "Audit-Ready" Driver Qualification (DQ) File System (score: 99)
- **TAM/SAM/SOM**: TAM: $940B US trucking industry revenue. SAM: ~500,000 small carriers (1-20 trucks) x ~$900/yr = ~$450M. SOM (Yr 3): 2,500 carriers x $900/yr = $2.25M ARR.
- **Growth**: Stable industry growth, but compliance software market growing at 12.67% CAGR, driven by increasing regulatory enforcement and digitization.
- **Fragmentation**: Extremely high buyer fragmentation. 97.4% of for-hire carriers have fewer than 20 trucks. This favors low-touch, self-serve models.
- **Margin pool**: Sits in risk avoidance. The cost of software (~$600-$1,200/yr) is an order of magnitude less than a single average fine for non-compliance (>$7,000).
- **Distribution control**: No single party controls distribution. Access is through targeted online advertising, content marketing (SEO), and presence in online trucking communities.
- **Incumbents and inertia**: Incumbents are either full-service firms (too expensive) or large fleet management suites (too complex). Inertia is reliance on paper files, which is a weak defense against the guaranteed New Entrant Audit.
- **Regulatory / platform risk**: Low. The business is built on top of long-standing federal regulation. The risk is that regulations change, but the trend is toward more, not less, stringent digital record-keeping.
