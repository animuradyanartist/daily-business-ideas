# Market: US Federal Firearms Licensee (FFL) Compliance Software
slug: us-ffl-compliance-software

## 2026-09-18 revision — idea: The Independent Firearm Dealer's "ATF-Ready" Digital A&D Book (score: 100)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM: 128,690 active FFLs. SAM: 53,925 Type 01/02 FFLs. SOM (5%): ~$1.6M ARR. (Source: ATF FY2024 Data)
- Growth: Mature, low-growth market. Opportunity is in penetrating the existing base still using paper, not market expansion. (Source: American Rifleman)
- Fragmentation: Highly fragmented buyer base of thousands of independent small businesses.
- Margin pool: Sits with software vendors and compliance consultants who can successfully mitigate the existential risk of license revocation.
- Distribution control: No single entity controls distribution. Access is through content marketing, direct outreach, and community engagement in online forums.
- Incumbents and inertia: Incumbents exist but the primary competitor is inertia (paper A&D books). The heightened regulatory environment creates a powerful trigger to overcome this inertia.
- Regulatory / platform risk: High. The business is entirely dependent on ATF regulations. A change in rules could render the product obsolete or require significant rework.
