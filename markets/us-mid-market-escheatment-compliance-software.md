# Market: US Mid-Market Unclaimed Property (Escheatment) Compliance Software
slug: us-mid-market-escheatment-compliance-software

## 2026-08-26 revision — idea: The "Audit-Ready" Unclaimed Property (Escheatment) Compliance System (score: 99)
- **TAM/SAM/SOM:** TAM is the multi-billion dollar risk pool from penalties. SAM is $375M (125k mid-market companies x $3k/yr). SOM (Yr 3) is $750k ARR (250 customers).
- **Growth:** Growing, driven by increased state enforcement and use of third-party, contingency-based auditors to generate state revenue.
- **Fragmentation:** Buyer base of 125,000 mid-market companies is highly fragmented. Supplier side is concentrated at the high end (Sovos, Ryan) with emerging smaller players.
- **Margin pool:** High software margins (~90%). Value is in mitigating catastrophic, non-discretionary compliance penalties, allowing for value-based pricing.
- **Distribution control:** Incumbents control enterprise distribution via established sales teams. The mid-market is open and accessible via content marketing and targeted digital ads aimed at financial controllers.
- **Incumbents and inertia:** Incumbents are focused on high-touch, high-ACV enterprise deals and services. Their business models create inertia against a low-touch, self-serve product for the mid-market, leaving a structural gap.
- **Regulatory / platform risk:** Low. The business exists to service regulation. The primary risk is the complexity of keeping the rules engine current across 50+ jurisdictions.
