# Market: K-12 Special Education Compliance Software (US)
slug: k-12-special-education-compliance-software-us

## 2026-07-13 revision — idea: The special education teacher's "IEP compliance" dashboard (score: 98)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM: ~$4B (Global Special Education Software Market). SAM: 13,303 US School Districts × $8,000/yr = ~$106M/year. SOM (Year 2): 20 districts × $8,000/yr = $160,000.
- Growth: 7-11% CAGR, driven by government mandates, rising prevalence of learning disabilities, and technology adoption to combat teacher burnout.
- Fragmentation: Highly fragmented buyer side with ~13,300 school districts in the US. No single buyer has significant pricing power.
- Margin pool: Sits with the software vendors who can successfully navigate the long K-12 sales cycle and then benefit from extremely low churn due to high switching costs.
- Distribution control: Incumbents like PowerSchool and Frontline control distribution through their existing SIS relationships. New entrants must use direct sales, conferences, and content marketing.
- Incumbents and inertia: Incumbents (PowerSchool, Frontline) are large and slow-moving, with products often criticized for poor usability. They leave a gap for a challenger to win on a superior, teacher-centric user experience, which translates to better data and lower compliance risk.
- Regulatory / platform risk: Low. The primary driver is the Individuals with Disabilities Education Act (IDEA), a deeply entrenched federal law that is unlikely to change. No platform risk.
