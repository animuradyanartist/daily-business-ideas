# Market: Small US Produce Farm FSMA Compliance Software
slug: small-produce-farm-fsma-compliance-software-us

## 2026-07-10 revision — idea: The Small Farm's "FSMA Audit-Ready" Compliance Locker (score: 93)
- TAM/SAM/SOM: TAM: Global food traceability software market ~$26B+; SAM: ~100,000 US farms at ~$499/year = ~$49.9M; SOM: 500 farms = ~$250k ARR.
- Growth: Market growing at ~9% CAGR, driven by regulation.
- Fragmentation: Highly fragmented. 85% of US farms are small family farms. No buyer concentration.
- Margin pool: Margin sits with the software provider who can replace expensive manual labor and consultant fees with a scalable, high-margin product.
- Distribution control: No single entity controls access. Distribution is possible through content marketing, SEO, and partnerships with hundreds of state and local farm associations.
- Incumbents and inertia: Incumbents (SafetyChain, Trustwell) are focused on large enterprise customers. Inertia (paper/spreadsheets) is the main competitor, but is becoming untenable due to retailer requirements for electronic records.
- Regulatory / platform risk: Low. The business exists to help customers comply with regulation. The risk is that the regulation is weakened or repealed, which is unlikely.
- Key sources: USDA ERS reports on compliance costs, retailer statements on deadlines, food traceability market reports.
